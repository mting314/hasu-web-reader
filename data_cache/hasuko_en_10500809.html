It's raining again today.

And it's already been five days since Izumi missed school. She did send a message saying "Don't worry," but...

Hey, Hime-chan. Is it okay, going to meet her old senpai?
Hime
Hmm...
Ginko
Maybe she'll return to the theater world just like that...
Ginko
And... if Izumi disappears, Ceras, who's back home for tests right now, might go to Mizukawa next year...
Hime
You're such a worrier, Ginko-chan.
Hime
...I'm sure it'll be okay.
Ginko
Huh?
Kosuzu
Hime-chan?
Hime
Just a feeling. Just a feeling.
Izumi
...Hello. It's been a while.
Ceras
Huh? Izumi...?
Izumi
I heard from Kairi-san that you were meeting her here.
Ceras
...What were you doing these five days? ...I was worried.
Izumi
You heard from Hime-chan, didn't you? That I was looking for the senpai I once did theater with.
Ceras
...I heard. Did you meet her?
Izumi
Yes.
Izumi
How nostalgic. The day I first met you was rainy like this too.
Ceras
...Hey, Izumi.
Ceras
I got checked at the hospital in Nagano just in case... and the results showed no problems. A perfectly healthy child. So things won't go the way you think.
Izumi
...That is good to hear.
Ceras
And, you know, I thought about a lot of things too. While you were gone.
Ceras
And...
Izumi
Before that, would you listen to me?
Ceras
...Mm. ...Okay.
Izumi
Thank you.
Izumi
My senpai kept doing theater.
Izumi
It seemed her body has not fully healed, but this time, she is properly going at her own pace. She looked happy with her new companions.
Izumi
She thanked me.
Ceras
...
Izumi
She said she is who she is now because I stopped her back then.
Izumi
...Strange, isn't it?
Izumi
I went to meet her prepared to be yelled at, to be asked why I abandoned her.
Izumi
After that, we talked about many things. How my world had been gray ever since I parted from her.
Izumi
How, being empty, I could only feel passion by making someone else's dream come true. How I had carried empty feelings all this time.
Izumi
When I told her that, she invited me again. Would I do theater with her once more?
Izumi
This time, it was a small dream. Within her own reach, with companions she loved, she wanted to complete a play she could think was the best.
Izumi
She said she was no longer aiming for the summit of dreams like before, and precisely because of that, she wanted me to come with her.
Ceras
Izumi.
Ceras
...What did you answer?
Izumi
...I said that might be good too.
Izumi
If I hadn't kept cursing myself all this time, surely that future could have existed too.
Izumi
But I have a new dream now.
Senpai
I see... I see!
Izumi
...Why do you look so happy?
Senpai
Because you found it, Izumi-chan.
Senpai
A new dream... a dream of your own!
Izumi
...No, Senpai. This is no longer only my dream.
Senpai
Huh?
Izumi
This is--
Izumi
Ceras. It is the dream you gave me.
Izumi
My dream called school idols.
Izumi
Without you, I would not have been able to move forward. And I would not have come to love school idols.
Ceras
Then, Izumi...
Izumi
Yes.
Izumi
The days with my senpai were truly fun. At last, I remembered that.
Izumi
Why did I think of them as a curse? They were so much fun...
Izumi
At last, I was able to settle my memories with her.
Izumi
From here, I will begin walking again.
Izumi
Thus it came to pass.
Izumi
Beneath the stars, the innocent princess's wish at last crystallized. Here and now, the tale plays its spring triumph and reaches its joyous finale!
Izumi
My knight and partner, Ceras.
Izumi
You made all of it come true. The journey to this point... was truly a great service.
Ceras
Izumi...
Izumi
...Was that a little too theatrical?
Ceras
...No. I'm happy. Really... so much.
Ceras
Thank you. Thank you for coming to love school idols.
Ceras
I made your dream come true, didn't I...
Izumi
I have only just reached the starting line.
Izumi
It may take an absurd amount of time to find a true passion of my own.
Izumi
Even for me, Izumi Katsuragi, whom people said could do anything immediately.
Izumi
But if I keep struggling to seize it by my own hand, I believe I will surely find it.
Izumi
Because I had my days with you.
Izumi
You, dreaming the dream of school idols, shone brighter than anyone. Bathing in that light made me think I could believe in myself once more.
Izumi
If even someone like me could come to love school idols, surely I can do anything.
Izumi
You will surely continue saving many people. Please show them dreams.
Izumi
I will keep thinking of you too, always.
Ceras
...Yeah!
Ceras
Hey, Izumi.
Ceras
I kept worrying and getting lost, wondering if this was really okay.
Ceras
But... your words finally gave me the courage to move forward too. It's a very large, tremendous dream... but I have a new dream now.
Ceras
The Izumi I saved pushed me forward.
Ceras
I'm going to meet Kairi. Will you listen to my resolve with me?
Izumi
Of course. Not as my princess, nor as my knight--
Izumi
I will respect more than anyone the words of you, my friend, who saved me.
Izumi
...Oh.
Hime
Izumin!
Kosuzu
Welcome back!
Ginko
Honestly. You finally came back.
Izumi
All of you... Why are you here?
Kaho
Everyone was waiting! For the two of you to come back!
Sayaka
So we came to welcome you.
Rurino
More importantly, what happened to Ceras-chan!? Don't tell me, again!?
Ceras
...
Izumi
Ah, no. It seems she got a little tired.
Rurino
Want me to take over?
Izumi
Hehe, I'm fine. Thank you, Rurino-senpai.
Hime
So, what are you planning to do now?
Izumi
For now, I feel like taking a bath.
Ginko
That's not what we mean!
Izumi
Haha, I know.
Izumi
I am sorry for worrying you all this time. Truly, I am sorry. I do not think an apology is enough--
Kosuzu
That's not it either!
Izumi
Huh?
Kosuzu
What are you going to do!? Ceras-chan and Izumi-chan!
Izumi
...Ah.
Izumi
We will continue properly, as school idols. Here at Hasunosora.
Izumi
Of course, only if all of you will allow it--
Kaho & Rurino
Yaaay!
Sayaka
There is nothing to allow.
Sayaka
You have always been students of Hasunosora, and our companions in the School Idol Club.
Sayaka
That has never wavered, not even once.
Izumi
...I see.
Ginko
...I'm so glad...
Kosuzu
Yeah, yeah, I'm glad. I'm so glad!
Hime
Happily ever after.
Ginko
Hime, did you actually know something?
Hime
Whaaat? I don't have a single secret from you, Ginko-chan.
Ginko
But you're good at dodging things, Hime.
Izumi
...Hime-chan, thank you so much as well.
Ginko
See!?
Hime
♪
Kosuzu
Ahaha.
Izumi
...Ah, light.
Izumi
...I wonder if someday I can become that light too.
Ceras
...Izumi just doesn't realize it.
Ceras
From the very beginning... you were the light of hope to me.
Izumi
...Heh, I see.
Ceras
........Or something like that.
Izumi
By the way, Ceras.
Izumi
If you were awake, I would like you to walk on your own feet. Or does my spoiled princess like my back that much?
Ceras
Wha!?
Ginko
Huh? Ceras, you're like a baby.
Kosuzu
Kachimachi liked being carried too! Until about kindergarten!
Ceras
!?
Ceras
P-Put me down! Put me down right now, Izumi!
Izumi
Ah, this is a good time. There is something I want to tell everyone.
Ceras
Izumi! Hey! Demon! You're listening, right!?
Izumi
It concerns next year and beyond, after Edel Note disbands.
Hime
Oooh?
Izumi
I want to continue being a school idol. But I want to try doing it by my own power, without leaning on anyone.
Sayaka
Meaning?
Izumi
So, Sayaka-senpai. Here at Hasunosora, I will become a solo school idol.
Kaho & Rurino
Solo!?
Ceras
Does that mean...
Izumi
No, Ceras. It is not because I think involving someone will make them unhappy, or any other negative reason this time.
Izumi
I want to find for myself the light each of you carries in your heart. I realized it is not enough to have someone else find it for me.
Izumi
At times, I may run into walls. I may stumble again and again. Perhaps this decision is not a wise choice.
Izumi
Even so, I want all of you to watch me as I try to move forward earnestly and stubbornly.
Izumi
Like Sayaka-senpai once did. Like Kaho-senpai, who raised up an outrageous dream. Like Rurino-senpai, who acted believing she could change.
Izumi
And like all of the 104th-term students, who recognized someone like me as a companion.
Izumi
I want to try my best so I too can become one of the lights called school idols. Standing shoulder to shoulder with all of you... yes.
Izumi
Just as Ceras tried to save me.
Izumi
I do not know if I can do it... But I want to try making my own flower bloom with my own hands.
Izumi
...Of course, I am fully aware that this request goes against Hasunosora tradition.
Izumi
If even one person is uncomfortable with it, I will withdraw these words. All of you are my benefactors.
Izumi
...What do you think?
Rurino
Go on, Kaho-chan.
Sayaka
Club President Kaho. It is your turn.
Kaho
Yeah!
Kaho
Izumi-chan! I think it's good!
Kaho
Do everything you want to do, Izumi-chan, and then!
Kaho
Let's all bloom lots and lots together!!
Izumi
...Thank you, Club President Kaho.
Ceras
Izumi...
Sayaka
...You said you were empty, just as I once was. I welcome the way of life you have chosen, Izumi-san.
Sayaka
Please make your own flower bloom here at Hasunosora.
Izumi
Thank you, Club President Sayaka. And everyone... thank you.
Izumi
With this, Bloom Garden Party will be our farewell performance, and the first step of our new dreams, Ceras.
Ceras
...Yeah!
Rurino
And Starring Bloom is next month! Let's do this, everyone!
Ginko & Kosuzu & Hime
Yeah!
Izumi
Hehehe.
Ceras
Yaaay!
