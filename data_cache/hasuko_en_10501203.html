PART 3

Kosuzu
We diid it, we did it, we went and did it!
Sayaka
Now, now, Kosuzu-san, please don't go too far ahead.
Kosuzu
Of course! On such a precious opportunity, I wouldn't go out of my way to separate myself!
Tsuzuri
That's true. It's been a while since we could be together like this, even if it is work.... I don't think I'd separate myself either.
Sayaka
Y-you do need to separate somewhat!? We're about to split up and hand out event posters to shops!
Tsuzuri
This is the Bloom Garden Party poster. It's well made.
Sayaka
Thank you very much.
Sayaka
Since the event is almost here, the plan is to have various shops put them up and build excitement.
Tsuzuri
Saya, Suzu.
Sayaka
Yes?
Kosuzu
What is it?
Tsuzuri
We kept in touch by phone and video calls, but... I still think you've changed.
Tsuzuri
Suzu became taller and bigger. Saya... let me see, I don't know if this is the right way to say it.
Tsuzuri
You've become very beautiful.
Sayaka
Wha....
Kosuzu
Kachimachi thinks so too!
Sayaka
...Tsuzuri-senpai, it seems you've become quite smooth with words....
Tsuzuri
Hasu no Sanrenka.
Tsuzuri
I'm happy. That Saya found a path of your own. I really think it's a good name.
Sayaka
Regarding that... yes. To be honest, I did have feelings of admiration for Hasu no Daisankaku.
Sayaka
But we are ourselves. The three of us decided we would become flowers blooming beside someone, not stars.
Tsuzuri
Your sparkle was a flower shining wet with morning dew.
Tsuzuri
No matter how many times it's stepped on, it keeps blooming. I'm glad I met that strength.
Sayaka
U-um.... Thank you... very much....
Tsuzuri
And, Hasu no Sho-Shihenkei.
Kosuzu
Yes!
Tsuzuri
After meeting a new star and changing shape, you're far stronger than last year, and shining by your own will. You had a good encounter, didn't you?
Kosuzu
I really did... so many things happened that I couldn't possibly tell you all of them over the phone!
Kosuzu
Actually... the one who first said, "How about Hasu no Sho-Shihenkei?" was Kachimachi.
Tsuzuri
Oh.
Sayaka
Is that so?
Kosuzu
Yes. I even had Hime-chan listen to me!
Kosuzu
I didn't quite have a chance to consult Ginko-chan... but I knew she would absolutely agree!
Kosuzu
This year, Kachimachi thought that connecting with someone is an amazing thing.
Kosuzu
So if we could connect with Izumi-chan too, I thought maybe we could do even more amazing things!
Kosuzu
Before, I wondered whether Kachimachi would end up holding people back....
Kosuzu
Those kinds of thoughts would cross my mind, so I couldn't really bring it up myself.
Kosuzu
But Izumi-chan opened her heart to us first. So Kachimachi wanted to answer those feelings too!
Tsuzuri
...I thought I had been watching the whole time. But I guess there are still things you don't understand unless you meet face to face.
Kosuzu
Ehehe.
Tsuzuri
Will you tell me? The path the two of you walked this year.
Sayaka
Of course. If it's all right while we work, as much as you'd like.
Kosuzu
Kachimachi wants you to hear lots too! Ah, but I also want to hear about you, Tsuzuri-senpai!
Tsuzuri
That's true. What should I start with?
Tsuzuri
Ah, there was something I hadn't told you two.
Sayaka
Oh.
Kosuzu
What is it?
Tsuzuri
I started working part-time.
Sayaka & Kosuzu
!?!?!?
Tsuzuri
Mmm...! This is it. The warmth that goes down my throat, all soft and hearty.
Sayaka
That isn't a burn, is it!?
Tsuzuri
I'm not that much of a child. I'm fine.
Sayaka
That's true... after all, you've even started a part-time job....
Kosuzu
Even Kachimachi was shocked.... I had heard that you were working hard living on your own...!
Tsuzuri
Though at first, I was only helping.
Tsuzuri
I found an oden shop nearby. I kept going there, but... one day, the shop was really crowded.
Sayaka
Don't tell me you helped out?
Tsuzuri
Oh, you figured it out. As expected of Saya.
Sayaka
Somehow... I can picture it going from there to the shop asking you, and then you starting to work there.
Kosuzu
That's such a Tsuzuri-senpai episode!
Tsuzuri
Then they asked me to come help a few times a week. It's fun. I'm always surrounded by the smell of oden. And I don't have to worry about meals anymore.
Sayaka
...So that's why, from a certain day onward, Tsuzuri-senpai stopped asking me about simple recipes she could make by herself.
Tsuzuri
Gulp.
Tsuzuri
A-at first I meant to try hard.
Sayaka
No, I think it's fine. Living alone for the first time in university must be difficult in many ways.
Sayaka
I'm only worried that your nutrition might become unbalanced....
Reika-san
It'll be fine. Oden is a complete nutritional food.
Sayaka
That said, a diet centered on fish cakes tends to lack vitamins and dietary fiber... wait.
Kosuzu
Reika-san!?
Reika-san
I somehow made it in time!! For Bloom Garden Party!!
Kosuzu
Wah! It's been so long!!
Reika-san
Yes, it's been so long, everyone! The full 104th class DOLLCHESTRA lineup is here.
Kosuzu
Yes! All of us are here!
Tsuzuri
Did you go somewhere?
Sayaka
Ah, right. Tsuzuri-senpai wouldn't know. She went around the world.
Tsuzuri
Ooh. The earth is always turning, even where I don't know about it.
Reika-san
Hehe. You girls haven't changed.
Reika-san
Take your time today! I'll add all kinds of extras!
Tsuzuri
Yaay.
Sayaka
Thank you very much, Reika-san.
Kosuzu
Please come to Bloom Garden Party too! Here's a poster!
Reika-san
Thanks! I'm looking forward to it!
Kosuzu
With Reika-san back too, it feels even more like a year ago!
Tsuzuri
I'm at university, Reika-san went around the world. Saya is Hasu no Sanrenka, and Suzu is Hasu no Sho-Shihenkei. A lot happened this year.
Sayaka
Every year was always like this, wasn't it?
Tsuzuri
That's definitely true.
Tsuzuri
When we were first-years, second-years, third-years... and this year too, so many things happened.
Tsuzuri
I left the birdcage and learned how wide the world is. But there was one thing I learned.
Tsuzuri
University and the oden shop are probably each just their own birdcages too.
Kosuzu
...Meaning?
Tsuzuri
I left one birdcage and entered the next. It was a little wider than the one before, with many warm birdcages.
Tsuzuri
Wherever life continues, there is always a birdcage. When I get lonely, I let myself visit under their eaves.
Tsuzuri
Everyone welcomed me warmly.
Tsuzuri
I realized the compass I received was something for traveling from birdcage to birdcage.
Sayaka
...That's a lovely metaphor.
Sayaka
How is it? Is your current birdcage comfortable?
Tsuzuri
I think each one has its own good points. Saya and Suzu aren't there, but I'm managing somehow.
Tsuzuri
There are many good people. Even now, I live supported by everyone.
Sayaka
I'm glad to hear it. I'm sure that's the result of your own character, Tsuzuri-senpai.
Tsuzuri
Yes. ...Ah, but you're not supposed to say that about yourself.
Sayaka
Hehe, that's true.
Kosuzu
...Tsuzuri-senpai.
Tsuzuri
Yes?
Kosuzu
Which do you like better, university or Hasunosora....
Kosuzu
Ah, no, never mind!
Tsuzuri
...Suzu.
Kosuzu
Y-yes!
Tsuzuri
Irreplaceable things only ever increase. They never decrease.
Kosuzu
........Is that so?
Tsuzuri
Yes. You'll understand soon too. You, who made a movie.
Kosuzu
...The people important to me don't decrease... but.
Sayaka
.... Tsuzuri-senpai had wonderful encounters at university. That is something to be happy about.
Sayaka
And no matter what exists in Tsuzuri-senpai's present, I have no intention of handing my place over to anyone.
Sayaka
So it's all right, Kosuzu-san. What you are to Tsuzuri-senpai will never change.
Kosuzu
...Yes.
Tsuzuri
Saya, how about you? In your heart, does Suzu not change?
Sayaka
That's obvious. The place Kosuzu-san has in my heart will always be Kosuzu-san's place.
Tsuzuri
...She says.
Kosuzu
...I know. I do know.
Sayaka & Tsuzuri
....
Kosuzu
Ah, s-sorry! I'm going to the restroom for a bit!!!
Tsuzuri
Saya.
Sayaka
I know.
Sayaka
...It's difficult, isn't it.
Sayaka
I wonder whether there is anything I can say. Can the one who is leaving say anything?
Tsuzuri
It is a matter of Suzu's feelings, after all.
Sayaka
Tsuzuri-senpai. I... want to leave something for her.
Tsuzuri
You've been giving her lots since the day you met.
Sayaka
...Then, at the end. At least so she won't be lonely.
Tsuzuri
In that case... maybe you should tell her the thing you most want to tell her.
Sayaka
The thing I most want to tell her....
Tsuzuri
I think there are many. I know. But I hope you find one ribbon that can tie those feelings together.
Sayaka
My one ribbon, is it.
Kosuzu
Please come to Bloom Garden Party!
Kosuzu
The 105th class's final... grand stage!
