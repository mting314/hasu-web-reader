PART 5

Kozue
...Are you two here?
Kaho
Ah... Kozue-senpai.
Sayaka
Tsuzuri-senpai... Why are you here...
Tsuzuri
...
Kozue
Kaho-san, Murano-san, you do not need to make such awkward faces. You were trying to look into last year for the article, weren't you?
Kaho
Huh, ah, um... Yes! That's right!
Kozue
I thought so. You have done nothing wrong, so please do not brace yourselves like that.
Sayaka
Thank you... very much. Um, were the two of you looking for us?
Kozue
No. That was not... why we came.
Kozue
...It seems we were too late.
Tsuzuri
You mean we were too late to realize it might still be left in the large storage room...?
Kozue
Too late to realize that someone might come here. I wanted to hide the things around here before that happened.
Tsuzuri
You're not throwing them away?
Kozue
...I will not throw them away. They are a warning to myself.
Kaho
U-um! This... um! Last year, you advanced past the Love Live! regional tournament, right!
Kozue
...Kaho-san.
Kaho
Y-yes.
Kozue
Could you hand me that?
Kaho
Ah, yes...
Kozue
Sigh... This is my failure. ...It is true. We declined the national tournament.
Kaho
Kozue-senpai...?
Tsuzuri
Are you going to tell them?
Kozue
These girls did nothing wrong. They only worked hard for the club.
Kozue
It is our responsibility that we failed to hide it. So rather than leaving behind a strange bad feeling, it is better to explain properly.
Tsuzuri
...Mm.
Kozue
Around this time last year, there were a few more members. Including Tsuzuri and me, there were three first-years. And then, just one second-year.
Kaho & Sayaka
!
Sayaka
Could that be the student council president's...
Kozue
...Yes. That senior... retired.
Kozue
Then, just when the three of us who remained were trying to keep going, one more person was injured.
Kaho
Ah...
Kozue
So... Tsuzuri and I entered the Love Live! regional tournament.
Kozue
But, you see. We could not match our steps. Tsuzuri and I.
Kozue
That is why we declined the national tournament.
Kozue
We had made it through the regional tournament, and I did think that since we had made it through, we should continue, but...
Kozue
Continuing any farther would only make both of us unhappy. I... could not stand beside Tsuzuri.
Sayaka
Ah...
Kozue
So we decided that we would not perform together anymore. But there is no problem with that.
Kozue
Tsuzuri has you now, Murano-san, and I have Kaho-san.
Kozue
I am sorry if we made you suspect strange things. But there is not a single thing to worry about.
Kozue
So... this conversation ends here.
Kaho
Kozue-senpai...
Kozue
Do not make that face. We are all right. Right, Tsuzuri?
Tsuzuri
...Yeah.
Kozue
Then please go back ahead of us. I will clean up here before I leave.
Kaho
...I did something unnecessary.
Sayaka
Kaho-san...
Kaho
I couldn't say anything. Kozue-senpai was definitely forcing herself.
Sayaka
...Yes. Both of them were.
Kaho
I said I wanted to do something... But all I did was make our senpai feel awful.
Sayaka
...
Kaho
Sniff.
Kaho
We said maybe there was something we could do... But really, we were just idiots who only wanted to know.
Sayaka
... I do not think that is true.
Kaho
...
Sayaka
I believe I understand very well that it was not simply because you wanted to know, after hearing how you felt before we went to the large storage room.
Sayaka
And I too thought that if there was something we could do...
Kaho
Sayaka-chan.
Kaho
Are you okay, Sayaka-chan...?
Sayaka
Of course I am not okay. I did not want to see the two of them make faces like that either.
Kaho
Then...
Sayaka
But I also do not want to see you make a face like that, Kaho-san. To me, it is the same thing.
Kaho
...I'm not showing my face.
Sayaka
Then, because I want to see you smile, Kaho-san. Because I want to see our seniors smile.
Sayaka
Because that feeling is the same, I went to the large storage room with you.
Kaho
...
Sayaka
They looked happy, didn't they? In that photo.
Kaho
...Mm.
Sayaka
Because we did not want to see our seniors make faces like that, the two of us tried to do our best, right?
Kaho
...Yeah. You're right.
Sayaka
Then your feelings were not wrong after all, Kaho-san.
Sayaka
And besides, for example... if we had written the article as we were, I am sure it would not have turned out well.
Kaho
...The article.
Kaho
...I see.
Sayaka
Kaho-san?
Kaho
I see. That's right, it is.
Kaho
We worked hard because we wanted everyone to know about our amazing senpai.
Kaho
My feelings that Kozue-senpai and Tsuzuri-senpai are amazing senpai still haven't changed.
Kaho
No, after learning all kinds of things, they've gotten even stronger.
Sayaka
I feel the same. Your feelings are not wrong, Kaho-san.
Sayaka
Did you perhaps realize something?
Kaho
Yeah. Listen, Sayaka-chan.
Kaho
We investigated all sorts of things together, and even though both our senpai are so amazing, we learned that something happened that made them make such dark faces...
Kaho
And I really did feel frustrated then.
Sayaka
Yes. That is exactly why we thought there might be something we could do.
Kaho
Right. We searched for something we could do. We tried to work hard by ourselves at something our senpai couldn't do.
Kaho
Then earlier, I couldn't say anything to Kozue-senpai. When she smiled like that and said this was as far as it went, I couldn't.
Kaho
I couldn't say, "But."
Sayaka
...Can you say that "but" now?
Kaho
Yeah! I can say "but" now. A way that we and our senpai can make together... a way to keep them from making dark faces!
Kaho
There was something the two of them couldn't do, and even if we tried hard on our own, we still couldn't do it.
Kaho
But the four of us haven't done anything yet!!
Sayaka
Kaho-san...
Sayaka
I see...
Sayaka
...You are right!
Sayaka
Whether offstage or onstage, the Hasunosora of today is not just the two of them either.
Kaho
Yeah!
Kaho
All right, in that case! We're already in pajamas, but let's change and go to our senpai!
Sayaka
Kaho-san. It is almost lights-out time today.
Kaho
Huh, but...
Sayaka
Shall we go in our pajamas?
Kaho
...Yeah!!
Kaho & Sayaka
Ahaha!
Kozue
Kaho-san, at this hour... and in your nightclothes, no less!
Kaho
Kozue-senpai! There's more to the conversation from earlier!
Kozue
Earlier...?
Kozue
I believe I said we should end that conversation.
Kozue
...Kaho-san, I am happy about your kindness, but that is not what I need right now.
Kaho
No! It is necessary!
Kozue
Wait, your voice is loud too. Oh honestly, if we are going to talk properly, let us do it tomorrow...
Kaho
Kozue-senpai, you said it yourself!!
Kaho
You said that the current Tsuzuri-senpai has Sayaka-chan, and Kozue-senpai has me!
Kaho
The current Kozue-senpai needs me!
Kozue
Kaho-san, what are you...
Kaho
To me, this School Idol Club is an incredibly important place.
Kaho
I worked hard at all kinds of things because I wanted everyone to know that we have incredibly appealing senpai!
Kozue
...
Kaho
I worked hard! Sayaka-chan and I are here, working hard for our senpai!
Kozue
I am happy about your feelings. But, as I said earlier, those are only feelings. That does not mean there is anything you can do.
Kaho
There is!
Kozue
...
Kaho
The Hasunosora Girls' High School Idol Club is not just Kozue-senpai and Tsuzuri-senpai!
Kaho
Including Sayaka-chan and me, there are four of us! So!
Kaho
For the Nadeshiko Festival live, let's do it with all four of us!! Let's do it together as four!!
