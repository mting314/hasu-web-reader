Well...

I'm sorry. Starting tomorrow, I'll listen seriously in class.

What I wanted to talk about today is--

Last year, I believed that was the best thing to do.

Megumi was injured, and Sachi-senpai was gone.
Kozue
I wanted to protect the club, and in the regional final, I hurt Tsuzuri.
Tsuzuri
...
Kozue
We clashed over our opinions again and again, throwing egos at each other that could never be called convictions.
Kozue
This is proof that we were immature, I suppose... a certificate of young trees.

If there is still anything either of you wants to say to me, I intend to accept all of it.

If I had been able to handle more things a little better...

If you had handled them better, I wouldn't have gotten hurt? I wouldn't have spent a whole year smoldering?

That is...

Kozue, you really are arrogant.

Megu.
Megumi
I got hurt because I got hurt. I couldn't dance because I couldn't dance.

That's all. There's no meaning in it.

But you two kept helping with my rehab forever, and tried to cut into your practice time and all your other time too.
Megumi
That's why I decided to quit being a school idol. That was something I decided.
Megumi
Can't you see? All three of our signatures are written right here.

You might be the club president now, Kozue, but to us, both the Kozue back then and the Kozue now are just a classmate who's only a little more put-together than the people around her.

...Megumi.

When I remember around this time last year, my body still sinks. Like the light gradually disappearing underwater.

If that feeling is called regret, then I've been regretting it the whole time. I wish I had talked properly with Kozu more. And with Megu too, of course.
Megumi
Mm.
Tsuzuri
Then maybe I could have taken over as club president now.
Megumi
I don't think that's possible though...!
Kozue
...Both of you.

Okay, that's the end of the gloomy talk! The three of us were young, and a lot of bad stuff kept happening! That's it! Any complaints!?
Kozue
But...
Megumi
I just don't like it! Because it feels like every time something happens from now on, you'll say, "I'm sorry about back then..."
Megumi
I'm happy now, so you don't have to keep dragging it back up!
Tsuzuri
I think Kozu should learn from someone a little lighter. Like clouds.
Megumi
Relationships, studying, school idols, it's all the same to you, Kozue. If there's something you can't do, you keep doing it until you can.
Megumi
You think if you can't do it, it's because you haven't worked hard enough.
Kozue
Th-That is... That is just how everything is, isn't it?
Megumi
If I were you, I'd break after an hour.
Tsuzuri
I'd break after five seconds.
Kozue
But... I do hate it. When someone looks sad because my strength was not enough.
Kozue
If I can, I want to become something perfect, like music.
Kozue
On a musical staff, if even a single component is missing, it is no longer perfect.
Megumi
Tsuzuri, pull Kozue's cheek.
Tsuzuri
Muni.
Kozue
Whaff!? (Wait!?)
Megumi
Now you're not perfect anymore! You've moved farther from your ideal!
Kozue
Honestly! I was trying to speak seriously! ...Really... hehe.
Kozue
...But thank you. My heart feels a little lighter.
Tsuzuri
Only a little?
Megumi
Can't be helped. In the end, Kozue likes taking things onto herself too.
Kozue
I-I do not... think that is... true.
Tsuzuri
Then what should we do with this certificate of young trees?
Megumi
Hmm, rip it to shreds and throw it away? If we keep it, Kozue might display it in her room and call it a warning to herself.
Kozue
B-But facing the mistakes you made in the past is necessary for growth...
Megumi
You really were going to do it!? When self-punishment goes that far, it starts to look like someone's hobby!
Kozue
It is not as if I want to do it!
Tsuzuri
Mm. For now, let's take it home.
Megumi
Since our school has nothing around it, at least the stars are pretty.
Kozue
I like this road home. You can see all kinds of stars in spring, summer, autumn, and winter.
Tsuzuri
Huh? Come to think of it, there were two sheets of paper, right? What about the other one? Did it only exist in my imagination?
Kozue
...I did not forget it. There just was not a good time to show it.
Megumi
The Love Live! entry sheet!
Tsuzuri
The one we saw last year too.
Kozue
Of course, it is different from last year. Last year, only Tsuzuri and I entered.
Megumi
This year, six people, three units!
Tsuzuri
We can enter as DOLLCHESTRA.
Kozue
Exactly. We will be able to do what we truly wanted to do.
Kozue
No, of course, the Love Live! I entered with Tsuzuri was educational in its own way, but... that is not what I mean.
Tsuzuri
That way of saying it sounds kind of like Saya.
Megumi
So the day has come for Mira-Cra Park! to repaint the world!
Kozue
If it is Cerise Bouquet, I am sure we can reach the greatest school idols. Just like the dream we had that day.
Megumi
No hard feelings no matter who wins or loses, okay?
Kozue
Yes, of course.
Tsuzuri
But Saya and I are the best.
Megumi
Is that a victory declaration!? You genius!
Kozue
Hehe. I can never tell if you two get along or not...
Kozue
...But I am sure it is an irreplaceable relationship. Because if any one of you is missing, it does not work. You are the Hasu no Dai-Sankaku.

Hasu no Dai-Sankaku.

Tsuzuri?

?
