PART 4

Ginko
I told you so...
Kosuzu
The wall of our great senpais was taller than Tsuzumi-mon Gate...
Hime
Fuffuffu...
Hime
But we saw a clue.
Ginko
W-we did...?
Hime
Challenging opponents at a higher level than us means we can't win by doing things normally.
Hime
We have to make use of every weapon we have and crash into them.
Hime
And then we'll bundle everyone's weapons together... and obtain a combined special move!
Kosuzu
As expected of you, Hime-chan! A student champion! When it comes to contests, you're the most reliable!
Ginko
...You're right. I said a lot of things, but I'm glad you're here, Hime. Thanks.
Hime
No problem.
Ginko
Well, that aside, we have to do self-practice too. Around here, maybe.
Hime
There are quite a lot of people dancing.
Ginko
Ah... I guess because the Love Live! competition is close. Girls from nearby high schools, maybe?
Kosuzu
Hey, hey, those people are really good!
Hime
Huh, their dancing is so good! Are they high schoolers too?
Ginko
No, no, surely not... Wait, the person at the edge... she's amazing.
Kosuzu
Wow... she might sparkle as much as great Tsuzuri-senpai... Th-this is incredible...
Ginko
Their formation is good too...
Hime
That's what we're aiming for.
Kosuzu
Mm. Then let's train especially hard on the part switches. Let's do it!
Ginko
I'll play back what we recorded.
Ginko
........Click.
Hime
Hmmm...
Ginko
...Yeah, somehow it's still not good.
Hime
Even though we battled the third-year senpais and everything...
Kosuzu
B-but both of you look so serious, and your expressions are perfectly set!
Ginko
No, you don't need to search for positives even at a time like this, Kosuzu...
Izumi
If anything, I think the problem is that you don't have a grasp on each other's strong points.
Ginko&Kosuzu&Hime
Wah!?
Izumi
Hello.
Kosuzu
Ah, you're the dance-talented lady from earlier!
Izumi
Hehe. You were complimenting me. Thank you very much.
Hime
Your coordination was super amazing! You're a great team!
Izumi
Team? Ah, they certainly were good girls. But I only met them today.
Kosuzu
Huh!?
Izumi
I had a little free time, so I joined in. Thanks to that, I worked up a nice sweat.
Kosuzu
You only just met, and you did such complicated formations...? This Kachimachi is astonished...!
Ginko
...Um.
Izumi
Yes?
Ginko
What do you mean? About the problem.
Izumi
Oops, sorry, sorry. I stuck my nose in where it wasn't needed.
Ginko
Ah, that's not what I mean!
Ginko
We absolutely have to finish this song. If you noticed anything, I'd like you to tell us. Even the smallest thing.
Izumi
...Hmm, hmm.
Izumi
I see, it seems you have some circumstances. Then I'll offer just one thing.
Izumi
Know each other's strengths and weaknesses. In other words, your strong points and weak points.
Ginko
...Meaning?
Izumi
If your understanding deepens, you'll naturally become conscious of movements that make use of each other.
Izumi
For example, like a single play. On stage, there are various actors with their own roles.
Izumi
What role should you play to contribute most to the troupe? If you don't know your strengths or weaknesses, that would be difficult to decide, wouldn't it?
Kosuzu
Yes, I think so!
Izumi
A stage is the same. I believe it's still a story everyone creates together.
Izumi
Ah, sorry. I'm being called, so I'll go. I'm cheering for you. Do your best.
Ginko
Each other's strong points and weak points...
Hime
...
Kosuzu
The way she compared it to a play was super easy for this Kachimachi to understand!
Kosuzu
Right, of course we'd want Tsuzuri-senpai to play a cool role, and Sayaka-senpai suits mature roles!
Ginko
In that movie, they made me play a cute role...
Kosuzu
But it kind of felt close to what Hime-chan was saying too.
Ginko
Ah, true. Making use of every weapon means the weapons we ourselves have.
Ginko
In other words, strong points.
Hime
Hm, I see...
Hime
Meaning even in a match, depending on where and how we focus, the way we grow changes completely...
Hime
All right, you two! Starting tomorrow, let's keep battling!
Hime
We'll train intensely so we can fully make use of each other's strong points!
Ginko
Ugh, so it still ends up like that.
Hime
Even so, we've already practiced a ton. For now, all we can do is struggle, right?
Ginko
...That's true.
Ginko
Ah, but let's lower the level a little! The third-years are too strong!
Hime
Fuffuffu, okay. There's no point if we lose before we can show our strong points.
Hime
All right! Let's make the sparks fly!
Ginko
...Isn't this just because you want to fight?
Kosuzu
Ah... maybe.
