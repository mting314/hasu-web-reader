Ginko
Ah, somehow this feels relieving.
Kaho
What does!?
Hime
We lowered the level.
Ginko
Yeah, I think we can win.
Rurino
What is this, rebellion? Rebellion?
Kosuzu
But they have Sayaka-senpai on their side!?
Kosuzu
The Sayaka-senpai who balances school idol activities and figure skating while continuing her tireless efforts every day, and as a senpai and as a human being, is made entirely of nothing but points worthy of respect!
Sayaka
What is this!? What is going on!?
Ginko
Whoa, she's becoming like Hime when she talks about MiraPa!
Hime
Nice one, Kosuzu-chan. Ginko-chan, you can't let her beat you, can you?
Ginko
I'm fine with losing that!
Hime
Okay, Ginko-chan, your turn. Please tell us what you love about your senpai in one hundred million characters or fewer.
Ginko
I'm not doing that!
Kaho
Exciting.
Ginko
Don't get excited!
Ginko
That's not it!
Ginko
It's a match, Kaho-senpai!
Kaho
That's what they said. Sayaka-chan, Rurino-chan.
Rurino
Mm, I see. This inter-year match is less about competing over victory and more like the match itself is the point.
Sayaka
Ah, so that is what this is. With the competition just ahead of us... Kosuzu-san and the others are working desperately in their own way to grasp something, even if it's unconventional.
Hime
Shh-shh, shh-shh.
Sayaka
In that case... ahem.
Sayaka
Very well! Unworthy though I may be, I, Sayaka Murano, shall face you all with my whole body and soul!
Sayaka
If you do not value your lives, come at me!
Kosuzu
Hiiiiii!
Rurino
Sayaka-chan, you're super in the zone.
Kaho
If you do not value your lives! She said! Say it again, say it again!
Sayaka
Why are the two of you pulling the ladder out from under me!? Honestly!
Sayaka
And then! What are we competing in!?
Hime
If we're going serious, we want to clash using our strong points and build confidence in no time. So...
Ginko
Wait. ...To begin with, what are our strong points?
Hime
Those would be... ummm.
Kosuzu
P-please wait a moment!
Ginko
What do we do...?
Hime
That means if we can find our strong points while competing, it's killing two birds with one stone, right?
Ginko
Ah, I guess that's all we can do...
Kosuzu
Okay!
Kosuzu
Sorry to keep you waiting!
Hime
If we're going serious, we should do it on the opponent's turf. Let's compete in whatever you senpais are best at!
Kaho
That's different from what you said before!
Kaho
Also, what are we best at...?
Rurino
Solo activities, cooking, and... taking care of flowers, reading?
Sayaka
Those don't have much to do with school idols...
Kaho
...Ah, then, me, me! I thought of something!
Kaho
Its name is... "Variant Karaoke Showdown"!
Kaho
Yay, karaoke with everyone!
Ginko
We didn't come here to play, Kaho-senpai.
Kaho
Of course! Okay, I'll explain the rules.
Kaho
After we choose one song, the three of us split its parts and sing it. The team with the higher score wins!
Kosuzu
N-now I see!
Kosuzu
At first glance, it looks like an ordinary game, but if we don't properly understand each other's preferred rhythms and ranges, assigning the parts will be difficult...!
Kosuzu
This training will help us discover our strong points!
Kaho
Fufufu!
Ginko
No, I don't think Kaho-senpai thought about any of that...
Kaho
All right, then it's strategy meeting time. Gather up.
Rurino
Waa.
Sayaka
W-waa.
Hime
What song should we pick? I know most of the famous ones.
Kosuzu
This Kachimachi doesn't know many recent songs... Ah, but maybe I can manage songs my older sisters often listened to!
Hime
Hm, hm. Then maybe a standard song would be good.
Ginko
If it's Showa-era popular songs...
Hime&Kosuzu
...
Ginko
S-say something!
Kosuzu
What do we do...? Should we do a children's song...? Or something we did in class...
Ginko
Uu.
Hime
It's okay, it's okay. This isn't the real thing. The experience points you get from losing are huge. Let's take it easy.
Ginko
Hime... you're so kind...
Hime
Of course, since we're doing it, we're going all out to win! Uoooooh, let's gooooo!
Ginko
And I was just moved!
Kaho
Yaaay! Total victory!
Ginko
I don't know them... trendy songs and stuff...
Kosuzu
B-but now we know what kinds of songs we usually listen to!
Kaho
Fuffuffu...
Kaho&Rurino
O-hohoho!
Sayaka
Perfectly in sync!?
Kaho
Senpais can't afford to lose to juniors, after all!
Kaho
I believe in Sayaka-chan and Rurino-chan! The second-years' bond is harder than a pumpkin!
Rurino
Wea~k, so wea~k. If you know your place, tuck your tails and hurry on home~~ Ah, no, Ruri's bad at this kind of thing...
Sayaka
Please don't force yourself to taunt them!
Ginko
Ngh... grrrr.
Ginko
I'm going to learn songs right now!
Kosuzu
Right now!?
Ginko
Give me thirty minutes, senpai! Then, as thanks, I'll let you taste defeat!
Ginko
It's a rematch! Kosuzu, Hime!
Hime
Wow, you're fired up.
Hime
I see... Ginko-chan's strong point might be being able to show the results of thorough preparation... what is it, seriousness? Maybe.
Kosuzu
Yeah, I'm sure that's it.
Kosuzu
I think the next one will be a good match.
Kaho
Bring it on! Today we'll sing all night!
Ginko&Kosuzu&Hime
Yeah!
Sayaka
We are going home before curfew!
