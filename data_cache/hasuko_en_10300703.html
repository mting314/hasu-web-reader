PART 3

Kozue
It has already been a year...
Sayaka
Ah, Otomune-senpai.
Kozue
Murano-san. Is something the matter? If you are looking for Tsuzuri...
Sayaka
Ah, no, I did not come looking for Tsuzuri-senpai.
Kozue
You didn't?
Kozue
Well, thinking calmly, I suppose there is no predicting where that girl might be during lunch break...
Sayaka
Ahaha. Um, excuse me.
Kozue
...
Kozue
So, what brings you to the clubroom?
Sayaka
Um, yes! I heard Otomune-senpai was here...
Kozue
My, you came to see me?
Sayaka
It is not exactly business, or rather! Hello!
Kozue
Yes, hello... What is wrong?
Sayaka
Ah, sorry, sorry! Umm! It is, well...
Kozue
Murano-san.
Kozue
I do not really know what happened, but if I will do, I can listen, so please do not be so nervous.
Sayaka
Ah... Ahh, I am sorry, look at me.
Kozue
Did something happen with Tsuzuri, by any chance?
Sayaka
It is not that something happened, exactly.
Sayaka
Otomune-senpai... um. What do you think of Tsuzuri-senpai?
Kozue
Let me see... She is a classmate the same age as me, and a fellow member of the same School Idol Club, I suppose.
Kozue
She can be a little absentminded... But I am sure you know that too, Murano-san.
Sayaka
Yes, that is true.
Kozue
And... let me see. She is beautiful, isn't she?
Sayaka
Huh? Ah, yes, very much so.
Kozue
When I happen to look beside me, her profile as she gazes vaguely off somewhere is so beautiful...
Kozue
Even now, I sometimes find myself watching her without permission.
Sayaka
What an enviable thing. Unlike you seniors, I am short, so...
Kozue
Hehe. That is why I thought I would tell you.
Kozue
Ah, I understand now. I know what you are after.
Sayaka
Eh!?
Kozue
You are going to put this in the article, aren't you? The article you and Kaho-san are making for the Nadeshiko Festival. The introduction to the School Idol Club.
Sayaka
Ah... Ahh! Yes, that's right, that's right! Yes! Y-you found me out, ahaha!
Kozue
Hehe. In that case, I should speak a little more properly. I am sure Kaho-san has gone to Tsuzuri, hasn't she?
Sayaka
Yes, exactly...!
Kozue
I hope Tsuzuri speaks about me and gives you something you can write in the article...
Kozue
Well, there is no point worrying about that. Hmm.
Kozue
...This may go without saying to you, Murano-san, but.
Kozue
Tsuzuri is an incredible girl. From the moment we met, whenever she stood onstage, she could captivate anyone.
Kozue
...No. Perhaps it would be more accurate to say that wherever that girl decides to dance turns into a stage.
Sayaka
Wherever she decides to dance...
Kozue
Yes. Once she dances, everyone's eyes are stolen away.
Sayaka
Ah... yes, I have experienced that.
Kozue
Haven't you? I met her at the entrance ceremony, so our relationship only began in high school.
Kozue
But for me, who had the dream of being active as a school idol... Being in the same club as that girl was truly a point of pride.
Sayaka
...You wanted to be active as a school idol?
Kozue
Yes. From the start, I intended to work hard as a school idol, and that is why I came to this school with a Love Live! championship history.
Kozue
...So in that sense too, Tsuzuri and I are complete opposites.
Sayaka
Huh, is that so?
Kozue
Yes, yes.
Kozue
Tsuzuri only learned about the existence of school idols around February right before enrollment, and said that any school would do as long as she could get in now and be active as a school idol...
Kozue
She was a girl who leapt in with that kind of momentum.
Sayaka
Being able to get in that way is very Tsuzuri-senpai...
Kozue
That is true.
Kozue
If that girl had known about school idols earlier and had tried to enter a school with a school idol club...
Kozue
She would have had more offers than she knew what to do with from the start.
Kozue
...Perhaps that is why. At first, I was irritated.
Kozue
She knew nothing about Love Live! either. I was the one who taught her from the beginning.
Sayaka
Um, about being irritated, did that work itself out?
Kozue
Hm? Ah. Something like that vanished the moment I saw her practice.
Kozue
Being with that girl was the best thing for me to grow... I was able to feel certain of that then too.
Kozue
Shining, stealing people's eyes away... To me, she is also someone I admire.
Sayaka
I... see.
Sayaka
Um.
Kozue
What is it?
Sayaka
I understand how you feel, Otomune-senpai. But if that is the case...
Sayaka
Why, until I came...
Kozue
Why did no one stand beside her, right?
Kozue
That is... I do not intend to explain in detail. But, yes. If there is one thing I can say.
Kozue
Admiring Tsuzuri and standing onstage with her could not coexist. Not in the past, and not from now on.
