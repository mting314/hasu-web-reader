PART 5

Kaho
This is... the School Idol Club's...?
Kaho
Um, thank you for showing me the wa--
Kaho
S-she's gone...!? Was she really a fairy of the large storage room...!?
Kaho
No way... Ah.
Kaho
Huh, is this...
Kozue
That is a photograph from when they won "Love Live!"
Kozue
Hasunosora's senpai stood at the very top of school idols. It captures that moment.
Kaho
Kozue-senpai...
Kozue
I heard from Tsuzuri. She said you might have gone here. Still, I am impressed you reached it alone.
Kaho
Oh, there was someone who showed me the way...
Kozue
That notebook.
Kaho
Huh? Ah, I'm sorry. It was in the clubroom, and I brought it with me...
Kozue
It is a little embarrassing, but... it is all right. This belongs to everyone in the School Idol Club.
Kozue
When I am lost, or when I want to organize my thoughts, I open the notebooks. When I touch these notebooks, somehow I feel at ease.
Kaho
At ease...?
Kozue
Even when I am worrying about how I can do things better.
Kozue
Because I can understand that the school idol senpai I admired worried like this every day too, and still did their best.
Kaho
...Even you worry about things like that, Kozue-senpai?
Kozue
Always.
Kozue
That is why I straighten my back and face forward, trying to at least look cool in front of my junior.
Kozue
So that what my senpai did for me, I can now properly return to my junior.
Kozue
So that future new students will tell me I am someone they admire.
Kaho
...Um, I... read a little of the notebooks.
Kaho
There were so many complaints and so much mutual encouragement... Somehow, they resonated with me too.
Kaho
Senpai. I went beyond the menu you went out of your way to make for me, and pushed myself too hard.
Kaho
So! Please scold me properly!
Kozue
Hinoshita-san...?
Kaho
Because you believed in me, Senpai...
Kaho
The fact that I practiced on my own means I didn't believe your words!
Kaho
That's not okay!
Kaho
So, Kozue-senpai. I want to keep continuing our unit together with you from now on.
Kaho
Please scold me properly here!
Kozue
...Yes.
Kozue
I understand, Hinoshita-san. What you did was not good. If you were injured, I would be sad. From next time on, please do not do it again.
Kaho
...Yes.
Kozue
Then next is my turn. I need you to scold me too.
Kaho
Huh?
Kozue
I forced the date of the live show on you, did I not? Because of that, you pushed yourself too hard.
Kozue
I should have faced your worries more directly, and the two of us should have talked things over properly.
Kaho
But that's not something you should be scolded for.
Kozue
Just as you made a mistake, I was not a perfect senpai either.
Kozue
What do you think? So from now on, how about we take each other's hands and walk together?
Kaho
Ah...
Kozue
Tell me, Hinoshita-san. If you are willing, that is.
Kozue
I may be an awkward, stubborn, inflexible senpai... but once again, would you be a school idol together with me?
Kozue
You and I, wearing these costumes.
Kaho
But... I don't have the right to wear that costume...
Kozue
Please, Kaho-san.
Kozue
Form a unit with me one more time.
Kozue
I want to stand beside you as you do your best.
Kaho
That's what I should be saying!
Kaho
I only just started morning practice recently, I don't have much stamina, and my singing and dancing still have a long way to go!
Kaho
But I want to keep being a school idol with you, Senpai!
Kozue
Wa.
Kaho
Kozue-senpai! I thought that if I worked hard, I could bloom on my own.
Kaho
But now, as a school idol, I want to bloom together with you, Kozue-senpai. Senpai, please!
Kaho
Please be a school idol with me!
Kozue
Kaho-san.
Kozue
...Yes, of course. And thank you.
Kaho
Senpai...!
Kozue
Let you and I make a full bloom of flowers blossom on stage.
Kaho
Yes!
Kaho
Senpai, is your foot okay...?
Kozue
This much is nothing.
Kaho
Really...?
Kozue
I was told to rest properly for several days, though. But if that is all, I can practice and quickly regain what I lose.
Kaho
I'm really glad.
Kaho
Come to think of it, a beautiful person came to visit you! Is she one of your classmates?
Kozue
...Yes, she is.
Kozue
But we will have to sign up for a new event again. We have to set another date. After all the practice we did.
Kaho
It's okay now.
Kozue
Is it?
Kaho
Yes. Right now, even if it isn't a big live show, I just want to do a live show. A good live show with you, Kozue-senpai, one that everyone will remember!
Kozue
...I see. Let us have a wonderful live show together, Kaho-san.
Kaho
Yes, Kozue-senpai!
Sayaka
There are so many people...!
Tsuzuri
Everyone was waiting for Kaho's live show.
Sayaka
She did work so hard every day!
Kaho
Nice to meet you, everyone!
Kozue
We are a unit passed down at Hasunosora.
Kaho & Kozue
Cerise Bouquet!
Kozue
Thank you all for coming today. We would be happy if you enjoy yourselves.
Kaho
We may still be like buds that have only just sprouted.
Kaho
But we will make sure you are never bored, and we will keep entertaining you forever and ever!
Kaho
Until the day we bloom splendidly-- please be our rain, our wind, and our sun!
Sayaka
Amazing, Kaho-san! You are a new school idol, and yet you are April's monthly MVP!
Kaho
Ehehehehe... Wow, this is embarrassing.
Sayaka
But it really was a wonderful live show! Right, Yugiri-senpai?
Tsuzuri
Ah, yeah... It was...
Kozue
You truly did so well, Kaho-san! I am happy that your efforts have been recognized!
Kaho
Wawa, Kozue-senpai! Ehehe! That's right!
Kozue
By the way.
Kozue
Why did you not come to morning practice today?
Kaho
Eeh!?
Kaho
W-well, we had the best live show, and everyone was happy, so I thought maybe I could sleep plenty for a while...
Kozue
I see. That makes sense.
Kaho
Ehehehe...
Kozue
But if you had the best live show, then next time you must do a live show that surpasses it.
Kaho
N-no way! Does the bar just keep getting higher forever!?
Kozue
Yes. You were the one who said you would keep entertaining the people who cheer you on forever, Kaho-san.
Kozue
Therefore, I will make sure to add the missed morning practice to the after-school training menu.
Kaho
Eeeeeeeh!? I-isn't that overwork!?
Kaho
You told me to rest properly too, didn't you, Kozue-senpai!? Right!?
Kozue
Yes, perhaps that might have been true a week ago. But you seem to have gained stamina, so this much will be fine.
Kozue
Ah, in that case, from tomorrow I could increase the menu a little more...
Kaho
I did say to be strict with me, but I think this might be a little too much!
Kaho
Help me, Tsuzuri-senpai! Sayaka-chan! Wait--why are you recording, Sayaka-chan!?
Sayaka
Hinoshita Kaho-san is working hard at school idol activities today as well, so everyone, please continue to support her.
Kaho
Nooooo!!! I only want to do fun things!!!
