PART 4

Sayaka
Ginko-chaaan!
Ginko
You're still doing that!?
Sayaka
How are the costumes after that?
Ginko
And somehow the imitation is getting more natural...
Ginko
Um, they're still difficult. I tried remembering when I went to see rock with Kozue-senpai in February, but...
Ginko
I can't quite see a vision for how to make costumes that suit a rock stage.
Sayaka
Mm-hmm. Then let's dig a little deeper. What do you want to do on the rock festival stage, Ginko-chan?
Ginko
What I want to do... I do think I want us to have a shine like those rock performers.
Ginko
They express something like a cry from the heart.
Sayaka
I see... Then let's cry out! All the things you've held back or kept inside, with full force! Come on!
Ginko
Here!?
Sayaka
Yeah, here! With the force of imitating rock, like I'm imitating Kaho-chan!
Ginko
That is persuasive in all kinds of ways...
Ginko
If so, maybe it becomes wanting to express us as we are... As we are...
Ginko
I see. Our unadorned selves, with no need to decorate. Sayaka-senpai, I think I understand. ...It's fine to be ourselves.
Sayaka
Did that give you a starting point!?
Ginko
Yes. The costumes should be fine now! Thank you.
Sayaka
That's our Kaho-chan!
Ginko
What emotion are you speaking from!?
Sayaka
Kaho-chan-style worry-solving!
Hime
Hmm, what should I do?
Sayaka
Are you troubled!?
Hime
Whoa! You startled me!
Sayaka
I'll listen to anything! It's a president's job!
Hime
Ahaha, thank you. Sayaka-senpai in this mode is fun...
Hime
Well, the start of all this was me and Kaho-senpai, right? I feel a little bad that Kaho-senpai worked so hard she collapsed.
Sayaka
That is...
Hime
For me alone to do what I want... it feels a little wrong.
Sayaka&Hime
...
Sayaka
No, no, no. I must awaken the Kaho-chan inside my heart!
Hime
Something started.
Sayaka
...Yes, Sayaka-chan may find this hard to say, but Kaho-chan would surely say, "Don't worry about that!"
Sayaka
Kaho-chan did it because she wanted to.
Sayaka
So you should do what you want too, Hime-chan, without holding back! Of course, you must not overdo it and catch a cold.
Hime
...That is true. Ahaha, Sayaka-senpai, you've gotten really good at imitating Kaho-senpai.
Sayaka
Ehehe, you think so?
Hime
But yes.
Hime
Following Kaho-senpai's example, I think I'll work hard for my dream too. So I can face the rock festival without worry!
Sayaka
Yeah, do your best!
Sayaka
Good, good, worry solved! Let's keep going!
Ceras
Um... Sayaka-senpai...?
Sayaka
Good morning, Ceras-san. Kosuzu-san too.
Ceras
! She's back!!
Kosuzu
Are you done imitating Kaho-senpai?
Sayaka
Hehe, no.
Ceras
Eek.
Sayaka
By becoming Kaho-san, I learned many things.
Sayaka
That experience now firmly exists within me. Therefore, I have not returned.
Sayaka
Right now, I am Sayaka Murano with Kaho-san.
Kosuzu
They're coexisting...
Sayaka
It means I possess both Kaho-san's mindset and my own.
Kosuzu
Kachimachi doesn't really understand, but it worked...?
Sayaka
Yes. I feel like I more or less understand now.
Sayaka
Kaho-san is very rock, isn't she?
Ceras
Now that you say it...
Sayaka
Her outlandish ideas feel that way too, but behind that outlandishness is Kaho-san's own strong desire to somehow do what is before her.
Kosuzu
True...! That was exactly how it was this March too! That's why we are here now!
Sayaka
Yes. I think I may have understood that feeling.
Sayaka
If everyone creates a Kaho-san in their hearts, the rock festival is sure to go well!
Ceras
Hinoshita Install...
Sayaka
Exactly! Now, Hinoshita Install!
Kosuzu
It even got a weird name!?
Ceras
...What would Hana-chan do, huh?
Ceras
I'm also unsure about the song right now.
Ceras
I haven't thought of a breakthrough... so I think I'll try it. Hinoshita Install.
Kosuzu
Even though you were so frightened.
Ceras
It's scary if it happens suddenly!
Ceras
What if Hana-chan actually isn't in her room anymore, and she's coexisting in Sayaka-senpai's body...
Kosuzu
What kind of horror movie is that!?
Ceras
Gradually, the boundary between them blurs...
Kosuzu
That's even scarier!
Sayaka
Your worry about the song, Ceras-san... was what kind of thing to perform at the rock festival, yes?
Ceras
Yes.
Ceras
No. That's right, Sayaka-chan, Kosuzu-chan.
Kosuzu
She's installed it already!?
Sayaka
Then I'll help however I can! I'm really looking forward to what kind of song it will be!
Ceras
Yeah, wait for it! I'll give it shape right away!
Sayaka
Huh!? You won't consult me!?
Ceras
I have to put all my feelings into it! Sayaka-chan, eat some katsu curry and wait!
Sayaka
I've never been told that!
Kosuzu
Kachimachi is scared of this space!
Ceras
Anyway... since I received advice, I want to try it myself. I don't think it would be good if it felt like I had everything done for me.
Sayaka
I see... The Kaho-san inside me looks like she wants to help terribly...
Ceras
Hehe. I understand that, but the Hana-chan inside me is brimming with motivation too.
Sayaka
I... see... Understood...!
Sayaka
I-If anything happens, please tell me anytime! Kosuzu-san, you were worried about choreography too, yes?
Kosuzu
Kachimachi could never install Kaho-senpai, but... like Ceras-chan, I do properly have the feeling that I want to try!
Sayaka
I... see...
Ceras
Hehe. Kosuzu-senpai has something close to Hana-chan, so maybe she doesn't need an install.
Sayaka
...True.
Kosuzu
That's too great an honor!
Ceras
But yes. Then let's do our best, Kosuzu-senpai. Aim for the stage where everyone can bloom!
Kosuzu
Yeah!
