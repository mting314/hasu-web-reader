PART 1

Kozue
And so, I would like to set our immediate goal.
Kaho
A goal?
Kozue
Yes. If we are going to practice, then naturally, you would want many people to see the results.
Kozue
It will help you keep your motivation, too.
Kaho
I see, that makes sense!
Kaho
Then how about we hold a live show around December two years from now, right before I graduate...
Kozue
Wait.
Kaho
But I still have so far to go! I need to practice a ton until then!
Kozue
This is quite a serious case...
Kozue
Listen, Hinoshita-san.
Kozue
If you could show a polished performance right from the start, that would certainly be ideal.
Kozue
But you do not need to sacrifice your own enjoyment for that.
Kaho
My... enjoyment...
Kozue
You love live shows enough to perform for a whole week straight. You would not truly want to hold yourself back from them for two years, would you?
Kaho
Uuu... yes, I want to do a live show...
Kozue
Then let us set a goal, and practice hard until then.
Kozue
You have only just become a school idol. At this stage, the more you do, the more you gain.
Kozue
Even a few days can make you look like a different person.
Kaho
Just a few days... Wait, is that when the live show is scheduled!? Isn't that too soon!?
Kaho
And this is a live event hosted by the city, right? Isn't that kind of huge!?
Kozue
It is an event where many school idols from around the city participate. It is a good opportunity for many people to see the results of your work.
Kaho
That does sound fun, but...! Oh, but! Look! Hasunosora students can't leave campus, right?
Kaho
So maybe it's impossible!
Kozue
There is no problem. If we have an official reason as a club activity, we can receive permission to go out.
Kozue
At this competition, the audience votes to determine the rankings. If we are going to participate, let us aim for a top placement... no.
Kozue
Let us aim for first place and do our best!
Kaho
Uuuuuh...! O-okay! I'll do my best! For a fun live show! Yeah!
Kozue
That's the spirit, that's the spirit.
Kozue
Now, here is the training menu I put together. Starting today, we will be doing intense training for a while.
Kaho
Aah, fine! Bring it on! Bring me arrows, shields, whatever you've got!
Kaho
...Huh!? U-um, Senpai... is this the menu for one week...!?
Kozue
Of course not. It is one day's worth. I compiled the menu I used to handle when I was in middle school.
Kozue
Let us start with about this much.
Kaho
...Maybe I'm actually fine the way I am now...!
Kozue
Did you say something?
Kaho
No!! I'll do my best!!!
Sayaka
Good morning. You are early today, Kaho-san.
Kaho
Uu, Sayaka-chan... good morning...
Kaho
You know, I'm doing "morning practice" for the first time in my life... Cutting into my futon time to move my body makes no sense, right? Hehehe...
Sayaka
I have been continuing morning practice for quite some time, though...
Kaho
Y-you have!? I thought I was the only person in the world doing it... That's amazing... Everyone's working so hard...
Sayaka
Hehe. You have been working hard lately too, Kaho-san.
Kaho
Well, yeah, I guess... Thanks to that, my muscles ache every day...
Kaho
I see, so you've been doing something this hard for a long time, Sayaka-chan...
Kaho
What a good girl you are... That's why you're so skilled, Sayaka-chan...
Sayaka
Please do not talk like a grandmother...
Kaho
Yeah, but I did decide, for now, that I'd do my best... So I'll keep doing my best, even while whining!
Sayaka
Yes, I will be cheering you on! If there is anything I can do, I will help however I can.
Kaho
Really? Thank you!
Kaho
Then maybe you could do morning practice for me...
Sayaka
Impossible.
Kaho
Yeah, I figured!
Kaho
All right, it's after school!
Ena
Kaho-chan, are you going to the School Idol Club now?
Biwako
Let's go together partway.
Kaho
Yeah, but before that, let's stop by the school store!
Shiina
The school store?
Kaho
The manga I ordered came in! I've been looking forward to it forever...
Kozue
Now then, Hinoshita-san. Let us go practice.
Kaho
Eeeh!? K-Kozue-senpai!?
Kaho
But, um, I need to stop by the store for a second! For, uh, daily necessities!
Kozue
If it is manga, the school store will hold it for you, so it will be all right.
Kozue
I see. In that case, why not make it your reward after the live show? Now you have another reason to work hard until then.
Kaho
I-I'll work hard even if you don't dangle bait in front of me! Just, please, at least thirty minutes!
Kaho
No, ten minutes, ten minutes is enough, so let me go first! Waaah!
Ena
I thought her senpai looked pretty and kind...
Biwako
Could she be... really strict?
Shiina
I wonder if Kaho-chan is okay...
Kaho
Hah, hah... hah, hah...
Kozue
Phew, then let us take a short break, Hinoshita-san.
Kaho
Kozue Senpaiii...
Kozue
I made a tonic drink for nourishment and stamina, so please have some if you would like.
Kaho
Waa, thank youuu...
Kaho
Thish ish bitterrr...
Kozue
Yes, but it is very good for you. ...If it truly does not suit your taste, you may leave the rest.
Kaho
Uuu... This is for the live show, for everyone...
Kaho
Thank you for the meaaal...
Kozue
Very good.
Kozue
I am sorry, Hinoshita-san.
Kaho
Hweh? For what?
Kozue
I was wondering whether I should have asked you a little sooner what you wanted to do. If I had, I think you could have practiced with a little more room to breathe.
Kozue
I thought I was doing the right thing by giving you freedom, but I cannot help thinking afterward that there may have been a better way...
Kaho
N-no, you can't!
Kozue
Huh?
Kaho
If you treat me that kindly, Kozue-senpai, I'll want to keep depending on you with no end!
Kaho
I-I'm happy! But no! Until the live show is over, please be strict with me!
Kozue
What is that? You say such interesting things.
Kaho
B-but if you're stricter than you are now, I'll get discouraged, so around how you are now is just right...!
Kaho
N-no, I think I would probably be okay even if you were a little stricter...
Kozue
Is that so? Then I will be even harsher...
Kaho
Eep.
Kozue
...is not something I would say. Overwork leads to injury. Even now, you are already quite close to your limit.
Kozue
Rest a little longer, all right?
Kaho
Whew...
Kaho
Oh, um, speaking of this event.
Kaho
We're participating as Hasunosora school idols, but only you and I are performing, right, Kozue-senpai?
Kozue
Yes. It seems Tsuzuri and the others will still spend a little longer in training.
Kaho
I heard from Sayaka-chan, but it's strange, isn't it? That there are lots of units within one school.
Kozue
Hasunosora originally had three units. That is why we are following that example.
Kaho
The Performing Arts Club senpai you mentioned the other day!
Kaho
Huh? If there were three units, then besides us and Tsuzuri-senpai's group, there's one more?
Kaho
But there aren't any other club members, right?
Kozue
That is true. At present, perhaps not.
Kozue
At Hasunosora, unit names, songs, costumes, and many other things have been passed down as traditions.
Kozue
The reason many people still cheer for us simply because we are Hasunosora is because of that.
Kaho
I see, so Hasunosora is loved!
Kozue
Actually, I too...
Kozue
Although they were from a different school than Hasunosora, there were school idols I admired.
Kozue
They were the first school idols I ever saw, who once saved their school from closing through their efforts. They shone so brightly, and I dreamed of becoming like them.
Kozue
The reason I came to Hasunosora was because, among nearby schools, it put the most effort into school idol activities.
Kozue
But before I knew it, I had touched the feelings of those who came before me here, learned what they had built up, and Hasunosora became precious to me.
Kozue
Now I am happy to be a school idol at this school, and it is my pride.
Kaho
Being a school idol at this school is your pride...
Kozue
Yes.
Kaho
Then I... I really do need to practice more, don't I!?
Kozue
Wa.
Kaho
Because Tsuzuri-senpai and Kozue-senpai are good, of course, and even Sayaka-chan is good, but if I'm the only one still lacking...
Kaho
Wouldn't that be embarrassing!?
Kozue
I believe I have said this before, but you have other charms of your own.
Kaho
But...
Kozue
I am sorry. Perhaps I said something unnecessary.
Kaho
Not at all...
Kaho
I just realized again that everyone has a reason to work hard as a school idol...
Kaho
And yet I...
Kozue
It may feel frustrating, but for now, take the menu in front of you one steady step at a time.
Kozue
For you, that is the shortest path.
Kaho
Uuuuuuu, I'll just do my best!!
