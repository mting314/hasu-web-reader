PART 2

Ginko
Progress... isn't going well?
Kozue
...I'm sorry.
Ginko
No, it's not something you need to apologize for!
Kozue
I should have consulted you much sooner... And yet I wasted precious time before the Renge Festival...
Ginko
I-it is pretty difficult, isn't it! When it's supposed to be the culmination of a whole year!
Ginko
And Kozue-senpai, I'm sure you're tired because Love Live! only just ended!
Kozue
It has already been a while...
Ginko
Th-then! How should I put it, you let your guard down! Like a weight was lifted from your shoulders!
Kozue
I see... So the feelings I put into Cerise Bouquet were only that much after all...
Ginko
No, that's not what I mean!
Ginko
Uuu... I'm sorry, I feel like anything I say will just make things worse...
Ginko
But why did this happen...
Kozue
...I don't know. This is the first time in my three years too...
Ginko
Kozue-senpai...
Kozue
...What if I stay like this forever...
Ginko
No...
Kozue
If Cerise Bouquet alone can't perform a new song at the Renge Festival... I would have no way to face all of you.
Kozue
In that case... It may be more certain for me to move into a support role and have you two make the song...
Ginko
...! That would be...
Ginko
But, um... This might be another unnecessary thing to say...
Ginko
But for this song, at least, I want you to make it, Kozue-senpai.
Kozue
...Is that because I'll be graduating soon?
Ginko
That's part of it, but...
Ginko
More than anything, I like the songs you make, Kozue-senpai. Delicate, somehow fleeting, but graceful...
Ginko
If they are to become Hasunosora's tradition and continue to remain even a hundred years from now... I thought I would want one of your songs, Kozue-senpai.
Ginko
So...
Ginko
Of course! I don't mean to leave everything to you. I will help with anything I possibly can!
Ginko
We still have time before the Renge Festival! As long as the song is finished in the end, that's what matters!
Ginko
We'll do something about practice time!
Kozue
...I see. So that's how you feel.
Kozue
Thank you, Ginko-san. I've received your feelings.
Kozue
...The truth is, I want to make it with my own hands too.
Kozue
It is the song that will adorn Cerise Bouquet's finale and close the curtain on my time as a school idol.
Kozue
After all... I don't want to leave any regrets.
Kozue
I want to properly convey how precious the path I walked with you, Kaho, and the three of us was to me.
Kozue
Words and feelings may be forgotten someday. But songs remain. Even fifty years later...
Kozue
And as long as a song remains, memories will surely keep coming back forever. That's the kind of song I want to make.
Kozue
For all of you. For the people who support us. And most of all... for the people who will come to love Hasunosora from now on.
Kozue
So, Ginko-san.
Kozue
...Please. Will you struggle alongside me just a little longer?
Ginko
Yes! If I'm good enough!
Kozue
...Phew.
Kozue
...Then let's do something a little reckless.
Ginko
I... I will accompany you...!
Kaho
Come in.
Kozue
Kaho, I'm coming in.
Kaho
Kozue-senpai!
Kozue
How are you feeling?
Kaho
I'm all better now! I can practice again starting tomorrow!
Kozue
Your complexion certainly looks good.
Kozue
Your fever is down too.
Kaho
Yes, because I was a good girl!
Kozue
I see. Very admirable.
Kaho
Ehehe.
Kaho
But now, I was able to thank the people who worked so hard for Secchan too.
Kaho
My Love Live! is truly, finally, happily ever after!
Kozue
...You were incredible in January, Kaho.
Kaho
Was I?
Kozue
Yes. The fact that we were able to win Love Live!, and the fact that we were able to complete the playoffs without regrets,
Kozue
your efforts were unquestionably a large part of that.
Kozue
To the point that I thought you might become the god of school idols, Kaho.
Kaho
If I became a god, first I'd want to build a shopping mall, a department store, an amusement park, and a Chinatown around Hasunosora,
Kaho
and lots of restaurants with delicious food too...
Kozue
Your desires have multiplied...
Kozue
You worked so hard even after Love Live!, so you can rest a little longer, you know.
Kaho
I can't do that!
Kaho
I heard about it. You're trying to make the last song for 104th-term Cerise Bouquet!
Kozue
Oh... I was going to keep it secret until you felt better,
Kozue
because I thought you might overdo it if I told you.
Kaho
I'm all right now! So I can't just keep lying in bed forever!
Kaho
I want to put every memory from these two years spent with you, Kozue-senpai, into it, and if I could, I'd make a song about four hours long...
Kaho
But if I did that, the live would end after one song...!
Kozue
It would turn into a play rather than a song...
Kaho
So I want to do everything I can to help make the best unit song ever!
Kaho
The beautiful woman named Kozue Otomune who once attended Hasunosora Girls' High School... I want her legend to be told forever...
Kozue
Wait.
Kaho
Students were captivated by her smile, flowers bloomed in her footprints when she walked, and little birds from all over the world gathered at her singing voice...
Kozue
It seems like you should rest a little longer after all...
Kaho
Kojue-senpaaai.
Kozue
It's not that I'm trying to leave you out. It's just that you're still recovering. For now, stay quiet, all right?
Kaho
Okaaay...
Kozue
And also... I'm not doing so well myself right now...
Kaho
Eh, really? Should we sleep together!?
Kozue
Not my health. It seems that, well... I've fallen into a slump. Songwriting isn't progressing at all...
Kozue
Just a little while ago, I had Ginko-san listen to me talk about it. Even though I'm an upperclassman. How pathetic.
Kaho
I'll listen too! I'll always listen whenever you want!
Kozue
...Yes. I truly am blessed with kind juniors.
Kaho
A slump is that thing amazing people get, right? So even you can end up like that, Kozue-senpai.
Kozue
...It seems so. I always thought it had nothing to do with me.
Kaho
But that's rough, when you're trying to make a legendary song...
Kaho
Hmm... A slump... How to get out of a slump...
Kaho
Then, then, I'll think of all kinds of things so I can help with that too!
Kozue
Yes, thank you. For now, I plan to flail and struggle.
Kaho
Does that mean you have some brilliant idea...!?
Kozue
Not quite...
Kozue
Tomorrow, you see, I've decided to go on a date with Ginko-san.
Kaho
Eh... Eeeeeeeh!?
