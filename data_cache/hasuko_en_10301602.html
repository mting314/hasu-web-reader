Sachi
Back when I was stuck, I often walked circles around the school.
Kaho
A change of pace... I appreciate the thought, but...
Kaho
Um, Student Council President. Do you have any tips for writing lyrics...?
Sachi
It depends on the person. How did you and Kozue make them up to now?
Kaho
Let's see... This time, we'd talk about wanting to make this kind of song...

Then Kozue-senpai would ask me all kinds of questions, and as I answered them...

Ahahahaha, that's no help at all!

Sachi
It'll be okay, Kaho.

You overcame that crisis in December. I believe in your school idol power!

This might be more of a crisis than that was!

Oh my, Kaho-chan? And... Sachi-chan too! What an unusual combination!

Hello. Reika-san, do you know the Student Council President too?
Reika-san
I do. She helped me with all kinds of events! Sachi-chan was even the one who introduced me to Tsuzuri-chan. Right?

Right.

So, what brings you here today?

Ah, actually...

Thanks to everyone, we were able to go to the Love Live! national tournament.

Ahhh! I watched it, the Love Live! finals! It was amazing!

Huh?

Wow! Thank you!

She did.

Um, this is an invitation.

Huh, ah. Th-thank you very much!

I brought an invitation from Rurino-chan!

Whaaat!? No, we're the ones who should be saying that!

--

Y-yes!

♪ Everyone was happy, huh.

Yes...

Me too... When I watched everyone's streams and stages, all that sparkle was so dazzling, and I thought I had to work hard too...
