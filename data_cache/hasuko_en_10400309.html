Why are you doing this...

...Yes. Even if my opponent is Ruri-chan-senpai, if it's a match, I can't go easy.

That's true now too.

Maybe I'd feel more at ease if I lost... Even so, it's something I decided to do seriously myself.
Rurino
Right. Yeah, that's it.

Mira-Cra Park! is the same for Ruri.

The same...?

Sometimes it's painful and hard, and I honestly think I don't want to fight with Megu-chan...
Rurino

Doing that wouldn't make Megu-chan happy, and it'd be rude to the people who support us...
Rurino
More than anything, I think it would be lying to my belief that "I'm going to do seriously fun things!"
Hime
Ah...
Rurino
Ruri was running away too. From facing you, Hime-chan.
Rurino
I should have known that if you won because someone went easy on you, you wouldn't be happy.
Rurino
So I'm sorry, Hime-chan. Ruri should have said this to you from the start.
Rurino
Ruri also has something she wants to do, and doing everything will be really hard...
Rurino
But I want to do it together with you, Hime-chan.
Rurino
Because I think that beyond overcoming hard things together, there's a wonderful view waiting that will make us think, "That was even more fun than before!"
Rurino
I want you to share the same feelings as Ruri by my side.
Rurino
Let's stop holding back with each other.
Rurino
It might be hard alone, so let's do it together on three. If we do, maybe we can become an even better unit.

...Ruri-chan-senpai...!

Let's become one together. The strongest unit, one that captivates the whole world!

Owah!?

Hime
But... But I thought even feeling frustrated might be presumptuous of me~!
Hime
I don't want only myself to have fun! I wanted to be of help to you two whom I love so much, really, really~!
Hime
I wanted you to let me help with Ruri-chan-senpai's fun things toooo!
Rurino
Ah, ahaha... I see, that's how it was...

Just like Ruri felt, you felt that too, Hime-chan. Somehow, we took a huge detour...

Uu~! Uu~!

There, there, there, there...

Ruri-chan-senpai~...

Eh!? Y-Yeah, got it...!

...Just enough that Ruri's battery doesn't run out, please...?

Uu~! Uu~!

She's saying, "Because we're teammates."

Eh? What does that mean??

...When hearts connect, does it turn out like that?

No, I dunno. But, you know, sometimes this kind of thing is nice too.
Rurino
Ruri and Megu-chan were... like that too, right? We clashed over lots of different things...
Rurino
Wait, could it be that every time you clash with someone, the battery cost with them goes down from then on...!?
Megumi
It worked that way!?
Rurino
I-I just thought it'd be nice if it did...
Hime
...Ruri-chan-senpai~.
Megumi
Ah, she talked.
Hime
I'll do my best not to hold back from now on too~... Let's do our best together, together~...
Rurino
Yeah, let's do our best together, together.
Rurino
Come to think of it, during the game this time, Hime-chan didn't go "uryaryaryaa!" Was that also because you were holding back?
Hime
? What's that "uryaryaryaa!"~? Do I usually say that~?
Rurino
You weren't aware!?
Megumi
Eh? Scary.
Hime
But that does make sense...
Hime
Ever since Ruri-chan-senpai called me out with a challenge letter, I was fighting while thinking, "Uwaa... I don't want to..." so...
Rurino
So it was because of your mood.
Hime
Ah, but from next time I'll be okay! After the revenge match, even!
Hime
For dozens of matches! Hundreds of matches! I'll keep Ruri-chan-senpai company! Forever!
Rurino
L-Let's play together sometimes, okay? Sometimes!
Hime
Ah............ I see...
Rurino
No, that's not it! It's not that I was holding back just now!!
Rurino
Ruri basically isn't very good at head-to-head battles that clearly settle black and white! That's why!
Rurino
Mmm, okay! --Then, Himecchi!
Hime
!?
Rurino
No, it's like, proof that we've become teammates... kind of!
Rurino
By changing what I call you from Hime-chan to Himecchi, Ruri can show her intention...
Rurino
Um, maybe that's a little too simple--
Hime
--No, I think it's good~!
Rurino
Y-You do?
Hime
Yes~! How do I put it, it feels like the distance between our hearts shrank all at once, and I can truly feel that Ruri-chan-senpai finally recognized me. I think it's a truly MVP nickname~!
Rurino
Th-That much?
Hime
Yes!

So, that's fine and all.

About the radio. I checked the broadcasting room schedule after all, but it looks difficult. Maybe we'll have to somehow get someone to give us a slot...
Rurino
Ah, about that, Ruri has an idea.
Megumi
An idea?
Rurino
Yeah, even if we can't secure a schedule on the day of the Nadeshiko Festival, we can still do radio.
Rurino
Ah, but starting now, it might get a little... no, pretty busy.

What do you think, Megu-chan? And Himecchi. Will you help me?

Of course, right?
