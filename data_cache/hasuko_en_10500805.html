PART 5

Kosuzu
Ceras has decided she will protect our nation, even at the cost of her life!
Kosuzu
O heavens, O seas, O this earth! Please watch over Ceras's journey!
Hime
Nice.
Ginko
Kosuzu, you're really fired up.
Izumi
O solemn providence of heaven! Behold, the beautiful princess born from the lament of our homeland!
Izumi
Now, at this very moment, she sets forth toward the far gate of fate!
Izumi
Dark clouds will thunder, rugged passes will bare their fangs, and every evil hand will surely leap forth to bar their way. And yet!
Izumi
Though hardships become walls a thousand layers deep, the princess will still draw the bow of hope, and pierce even the depths of night with dawn!
Izumi
Her name shall be etched into the future, and one day the people shall sing aloud: "She is none other than the princess who saved the nation!"
Ginko
...!
Kosuzu & Hime
Oooh...
Izumi
Whew... Hmm? What's wrong?
Ginko
No, it just had so much force... I got pulled in before I knew it...
Kosuzu
That was amazing, Izumi-chan! You felt incredibly professional!
Izumi
Did I? Thank you.
Hime
Wow, you really are good... So this is the champion of the theater world.
Izumi
It's just an old skill coming back to me.
Kosuzu
But it really was amazing!
Kosuzu
Even the script, when we were all talking about "this kind of story would be good," Izumi-chan wrote it up in just one day!
Ginko
When it comes to theater, you can do anything, can't you, Izumi?
Izumi
That's not true. I learned each thing desperately, one by one. That's all.
Hime
Look, look, the hallway is amazing.
Kosuzu
Huh...? Whoa! When did we get such a big gallery!?
Ginko
Everyone came to see Izumi...
Kosuzu
The drama club people are somehow collapsing!?
Hime
Ahaha. When Izumin hits them with her full power, everyone gets knocked out.
Izumi
How embarrassing.
Izumi
...Ah, it's been a long time since I felt this.
Hime
Izumin?
Izumi
Yes. Let's run through it from the beginning one more time. There are still parts I'm not quite satisfied with.
Ginko
Even after that...!?
Hime
Totally fine! Let's do it!
Kosuzu
Yeah, let's do it! So it reaches Ceras-chan working hard in Nagano too!
Izumi
Thank you. Then everyone, return to your positions...
Ginko
It's gotten completely dark.
Hime
I want to go home and recharge with sweets...
Izumi
Everyone, thank you for staying with me until so late.
Kosuzu
Not at all! More importantly, thank you for giving us so much advice!
Kosuzu
I'm glad you have experience, Izumi-chan. I have a feeling we'll make something amazing!
Izumi
To do that, our lead, Kosuzu-san, needs to practice a little more.
Kosuzu
Ugh! I-I'll do my best! Chest, chest, chest!
Hime
A triple chest of spirit.
Izumi
Hehehe.
Izumi
Oh... A message from Ceras.
Hime
Let's see.
Kosuzu
Wow, another photo shoot today!
Ginko
I wonder if they're making unreasonable demands again.
Kosuzu
But somehow, she looks like she's having fun.
Hime
Cerarin is the type to go past her body's limits once she gets absorbed in something. And right now, on top of that, she's carrying a sense of mission and responsibility.
Izumi
...Yes. I'll be careful. Passion sometimes turns into a runaway force. That happens often enough.
Ceras
Hey, Izumi. There's something I want to talk about.
Ceras
Actually, never mind.
Izumi
...Hmm?
Ginko
...Hey, Izumi, why did you quit theater?
Izumi
Hm?
Ginko
No, how do I put it... You looked like you were enjoying practice. I thought you must have liked theater.
Izumi
...Yes, I did like it. I had feelings invested in it too.
Ginko
I was wondering what those "unavoidable circumstances" were.
Izumi
...
Ginko
Ah, no, I'm not trying to force you to talk about it.
Izumi
I don't particularly mind talking about it itself.
Hime
Hmm?
Izumi
But I don't think it will be a very pleasant story to hear.
Kosuzu
But Kachimachi wants to hear it. If I can learn more about you, I think we can definitely become much, much closer.
Izumi
...I see.
Izumi
...It is unexpectedly nice to have you try to step closer to me like that.
Izumi
Apparently, I have opened my heart to all of you more than I thought.
Izumi
Will you listen? To my past, which I have never told anyone else.
Kosuzu
Yeah.
Izumi
The first dream I ever chased was theater. And there, I met the person who would change my fate.
Izumi
...And that is why I quit theater.
Ginko
You shared the same dream with your "senpai"...
Kosuzu
So that happened...
Hime
...Losing a friend you shared a dream with is lonely.
Izumi
To me, it is over. I'm sure we simply should never have met.
Ginko
I don't think that's true...
Kosuzu
But you didn't want to leave your senpai, right, Izumi-chan!? If she hadn't hurt herself, surely you'd still be together...!
Izumi
...Who knows. All I can say for certain now is that such a future never came. That is all.
Hime
...
Izumi
After that... after I let go of my senpai's hand, I managed to keep living by granting the dreams of girls who wished for dreams.
Ginko
But then... Isn't doing theater even now painful for you...?
Izumi
...That is the strange part.
Izumi
Until now, I felt nothing.
Izumi
Even if I helped with theater, not a ripple stirred in my heart. The world I lived in after losing my senpai had always been gray.
Izumi
But creating a play with all of you, to lead Zuiren Festival to success... I can think of it as fun.
Kosuzu
That means...!
Izumi
I don't know. But if there is at least one thing I can say, it is that after coming to Hasunosora, I seem to have been changing little by little.
Izumi
I chose this place thinking it would merely be a way to pass the time...
Kosuzu
I get it, Izumi-chan! It's "encounters"!
Kosuzu
Kachimachi thinks she was able to change because she came here and met Sayaka-senpai and Tsuzuri-senpai, and Ginko-chan and Hime-chan, and our senpai, and everyone!
Kosuzu
It's only a little at a time... but even if it's little by little, I feel like I'm moving forward!
Izumi
...Kosuzu-san.
Hime
Meeting MiraPa! changed my life too.
Hime
If we were able to have even the tiniest good influence on you, Izumin, that's better than anything.
Izumi
...Yes, Hime-chan.
Ginko
But the biggest one is Ceras, of course, right?
Ginko
She's been excited about Edel Note with you this whole time. Saying she'd "make you love school idols."
Izumi
...I thought that was only an imitation, shining because it basked in Ceras's light.
Izumi
That, just like always, it was bound not to work out. But...
Izumi
What do you think? Do you think the me I am now is shining, even a little, by my own power?
Kosuzu
You're shining so much, Izumi-chan!
Hime
There's no way an Izumin who makes that many people have fun at lives isn't shining.
Ginko
I think you're a splendid school idol, and a rival. At least, I do.
Izumi
...Thank you.
Izumi
When all of you affirm me, my heart feels lighter. It seems I had even forgotten something so obvious.
Izumi
Let's make the play a success for Zuiren Festival. I will do everything I can too.
Hime
For Cerarin too.
Kosuzu
Yeah!
Ginko
For that cheeky and cute junior of ours.
Izumi
Yes. For Ceras, who is struggling to save me.
Izumi
Indeed... I will accomplish this revenge and take it back... My passion.
