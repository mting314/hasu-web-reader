Kairi
Well then, good work today!
Ceras
Good work...
Kairi
Please let me know next time you come to Nagano! There are still many things I'd like to ask of Ceras-senpai!
Ceras
Oooh...
Kairi
Ah, sorry, I have a phone call... Excuse me!
Izumi
Whew. You worked hard today, Ceras.
Ceras
Weeeey...
Hime
Wow, her battery's dead.
Ginko
This is the first time I've seen Ceras-san being so serious all day.
Ceras
Apparently there will still be more photo shoots, interviews, an autobiography, and things like that...
Kosuzu
An autobiography!?
Ceras
But this is also for Mizukawa... I am the saint who saves Mizukawa...
Ginko
The photo shoot mindset has been carved into her...
Izumi
For now, Ceras and I intend to come every day as much as possible, but the rest of you shouldn't push yourselves.
Ginko
Every day? After school on weekdays, every day...?
Izumi
When I said that was my intent, Ceras seemed to want to do her best too.
Ceras
Weeeey.
Hime
You're both motivated.
Ginko
Then we'll come as much as we can too. If we can help even a little.
Kosuzu
We told our senpai to leave it to us, after all.
Izumi
Thank you, everyone.
Ceras
Thank yooou...
Kairi
Uuugh... I'm sorry, but something troublesome has happened...
Izumi
What's wrong?
Kairi
The circle we had asked to perform a play at Zuiren Festival had something come up and can no longer appear...
Izumi
...
Hime
What kind of play were you planning?
Kairi
It was, as Katsuragi-senpai said earlier, a play to show that Mizukawa's history continues, or rather...
Kairi
A fantasy themed around Mizukawa's revival...
Kosuzu
Wow, that sounds good! If you had that, people really could feel that the history is continuing!
Kairi
Yes... But the schedule is so sudden that it seems a little difficult to ask someone else now...
Izumi
...Hmm.
Ginko
If it was a play meant to show that the tradition still continues...
Hime
Just filling the schedule hole with a live show or something wouldn't work, huh.
Kairi
...Ah, no, I'm sorry. I'll think of another plan!
Kairi
I'll explain the circumstances to the backstage volunteers who gathered and have them disband...
Izumi
In that case.
Izumi
What if we performed the play instead?
Kairi
Huh...!? Would that be okay!?
Izumi
In fact, a play performed by students who were present for the school closure would be able to emphasize the connection even more strongly.
Izumi
If we aim beyond merely making Zuiren Festival succeed, it would be effective.
Ginko
Come to think of it, Izumi-san, you used to do theater, right?
Kosuzu
The drama club people said so too! That you were really famous!
Izumi
Well, yes. Though I quit due to unavoidable circumstances.
Ginko
Circumstances...?
Izumi
I ended up carrying a trauma that is, shall we say, not light.
Kosuzu
Huh!?
Hime
...Will you be okay?
Izumi
I don't know.
Ginko
Y-You don't know...!
Izumi
But when I look back on my resolve to challenge the Second Zuiren Festival, this also seems like just the right opportunity.
Ceras
...Revenge!
Izumi
Exactly.
Izumi
Since we're here, let's burn away every regret. Without caring how it looks, I'll put my feelings into it.
Izumi
If I do that, surely something will be born. Far more than if I do nothing.
Izumi
So, what do you think? If everyone is all right with it.
Kosuzu
If Izumi-chan says that, Kachimachi wants to try!
Kosuzu
I was thinking! What if we did a play about the Love Live! finals?
Kosuzu
We could recreate last year's finals. And have a live performance partway through.
Hime
But then Mizukawa would lose, wouldn't they?
Kosuzu
We can arrange that part a little!
Kosuzu
Make the main story about how hard Ceras-chan worked for Mizukawa!
Hime
Oooh. If we're going that far, maybe we could arrange it even more and actually make a story about the saint who saved the nation. Musical style.
Kosuzu
Oh, that might be better!
Ginko
Ceras-san is playing a princess again.
Izumi
But Ceras will have many things to do for Mizukawa, so in that case... Kosuzu-san would be best for the princess role.
Kosuzu
Huh, Kachimachi will!?
Hime
Sounds good to me.
Ginko
Well, nobody but Kosuzu could play Ceras-san.
Kosuzu
Y-Yeah, got it! Kachimachi will do her best as the princess!
Ceras
To think Kosuzu-senpai would play me... It is the height of honor...
Kosuzu
I'll practice not smiling too!
Ginko
So she's going with that character.
Kairi
That helps so much! Thank you!
Kairi
I'll contact everyone right away!
Kairi
I'll excuse myself for today! Thank you again!
Hime
But it's rare for you to suggest something like that, Izumin.
Izumi
It is. But once in a while should be fine, shouldn't it?
Hime
I like Izumin when she's motivated.
Ginko
Your acting was amazing during Hasunosora Fantasy too. It's reassuring.
Kosuzu
Yeah, yeah! Even when you have a free moment, I'd like you to give us some advice!
Izumi
Leave it to me.
Izumi
However, I do carry the name Izumi Katsuragi. So this cannot be half-hearted.
Ginko
Wow, you got pompous all of a sudden.
Kosuzu
I'll do my best!
Hime
Yeah, yeah. Now we're one big step closer to Zuiren Festival's success... no, to our stretch goal.
Izumi
Oh... This is how things have turned out, but I'm sorry, Ceras. If I'm to rehearse seriously, it seems I'll have a hard time commuting to Nagano for a while.
Ceras
No.
Ceras
Kairi is here on this side, so I'll be all right. But you owe me one, okay?
Izumi
Of course. I'll repay that debt by making the Second Zuiren Festival succeed.
Ceras
...Thanks, Izumi.
Ceras
Senpai, thank you as well.
Kosuzu
Huh?
Ceras
No, once again... Mizukawa is an important place to me.
Ceras
But everyone from Hasunosora is cooperating with me so kindly.
Ceras
The third-year senpai who couldn't come here are taking on Starring Bloom preparations for our share too, and working hard for us.
Ceras
Even though I can't repay anything... I'm very happy.
Hime
Ceeeraaarin.
Ceras
Wah?
Hime
What's this? Why so formal all of a sudden?
Kosuzu
That's right, Ceras-chan! Ceras-chan is our very, very cute junior!
Ceras
Hime-senpai, Kosuzu-senpai...
Ginko
Exactly. If you need a reason, it's "because we're your senpai." That's enough.
Ginko
At least, that's what the senpai who saved me said.
Ginko
Well, there is just one thing I'm curious about.
Ceras
...What is it?
Ginko
Um... If your dream of Mizukawa's revival comes true...
Hime
...You're wondering whether Cerarin and Izumin will transfer to Mizukawa?
Kosuzu
Ah...
Izumi
...
Ceras
I...
Ceras
...No. I won't return to Mizukawa.
Ceras
Right now, there are things I properly want to do at Hasunosora.
Ceras
I want to chase dreams together with Hana-chan and everyone, and make Bloom Garden Party succeed.
Kosuzu
Ceras-chan...!
Ceras
Mizukawa is important to me. If Mizukawa can be reclaimed once more, I want to do anything I can.
Ceras
I spent three years of junior high there, and had so many encounters at that school... I want to meet it again.
Ceras
Mizukawa is packed full of precious memories for me. But Hasunosora is just as precious.
Ceras
Because the me I am now is Ceras of Hasunosora Girls' Academy School Idol Club.
Izumi
So she says.
Ginko
Yeah... I'm glad. Ah, no, I don't know if it's okay to say I'm glad.
Hime
I think it's fine. "I'm glad."
Ceras
...
Kosuzu
What about you, Izumi-chan!?
Izumi
I have no plans to return either. From the start, I intended my association with Ceras to last only one year.
Izumi
At this point, I have no lingering attachment to Mizukawa. Only the regret that I couldn't help it remains.
Izumi
And if we can make the Second Zuiren Festival succeed, I am sure that will be sublimated too.
Hime
Hehehe, I see.
Hime
I can't imagine the School Idol Club without Izumin and Cerarin anymore either.
Kosuzu
Yeah!
Ceras
...I'm happy to hear my senpai say that. I'll work hard to become an even better junior.
Ginko
Somehow she feels distant again.
Ceras
Ah, no, this is...
Ginko
You can be like usual. Right now, you're Ceras of Hasunosora Girls' Academy School Idol Club, aren't you?
Ginko
...Right, Ceras?
Ceras
Ginko...
Ginko
I knew you'd say that!
Ceras
I-It slipped out! No, I was happy! It just slipped out!
Kosuzu
Ahaha. Ceras-chan was busy all day today and didn't have time to be silly!
Hime
Good for you, Cerarin.
Ceras
Senpai Ginko-senpai senpai Ginko-sen-senpai-pai!
Ginko
That's too many senpais, Ceras!
Ceras
How about that!? Izumi! Ginko-senpai called me without honorifics!?
Izumi
I'm jealous.
Ceras
Well, you can't do that, Izumi! Because you're not Ginko-senpai's junior! Right!
Izumi
How nice. How nice.
Ginko
Wh-What...?
Izumi
As a transfer student, couldn't I be considered something like a junior too?
Ginko
No, Izumi-san has, like, dignity...
Izumi
Ginko-senpai.
Ginko
Ah, fine, I get it already! Izumi!
Hime
Oooh.
Izumi
Yes, that feels right, Ginko.
Ginko
I-Is that so...
Izumi
Hime.
Hime
Ehehe.
Izumi
Kosuzu-san.
Kosuzu
Huh!? Huh!?
Kosuzu
Why!? Wasn't Kachimachi close with you yet!?
Izumi
No, how should I put this... I particularly respect you, Kosuzu-san.
Izumi
I revere you as a person.
Kosuzu
Huh, I'm happy, but why!?
Izumi
If I had met Ginko and Hime-chan earlier, when they were chasing their dreams alone in solitude, they might have become my princesses.
Izumi
But only you, Kosuzu-san, are, how should I say it.
Izumi
No matter how much I tried to reach out my hand, I feel like you would say, "That's something I want to do," and brush my hand aside.
Kosuzu
Huh, I don't get it!
Hime
Ah.
Ginko
Well, Kosuzu does have that side to her...
Kosuzu
I do!?
Izumi
Though you are the complete opposite of me in every way, you chase your dreams recklessly and continue challenging yourself. Looking at your back, I hope someday I can catch up to you too.
Kosuzu
Kachimachi was the one being chased!?
Izumi
That is why, with that in mind, I would very much like to call you Kosuzu-san with respect.
Kosuzu
It's okay to take that as meaning the emotional distance is about the same as with Ginko-chan and Hime-chan, right!?
Izumi
Of course. Thank you for always being so friendly with me.
Kosuzu
Same to you! Let's do our best together on the play challenge!
Izumi
Yes, let's make it a challenge with no regrets.
Ginko
If we bring a story this fun back with us, Kaho-senpai is going to throw another tantrum.
Hime
I can totally picture it.
Kosuzu
Ahaha!
Izumi
Hasunosora is a good place. Truly.
Ceras
...Yeah.
Izumi
Then let's go home for today, princess. You must be tired. When we get to the dorm, I'll give you a massage.
Ceras
Mm... That's the opposite of how it used to be.
Izumi
Indeed. If you wish, I can dry your hair too.
Ceras
You may.
Ginko
Izumi, Ceras!
Kosuzu
Hurry, hurry! The Shinkansen's coming!
Izumi
Yes.
Ceras
We're coming!
Izumi
You shouldn't push yourself too hard either, Ceras.
Ceras
You too, Izumi!
Izumi
And don't neglect your studies.
Ceras
Ugh...! Th-Then please help me, Izumi-senpai...!!
Izumi
Heh, ahahaha.
