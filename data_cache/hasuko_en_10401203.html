PART 3

Kozue
I'm sorry. Did I keep you waiting?
Ginko
N-no! I just got here!
Kozue
Will you spend the whole day with me today?
Ginko
Y-yes!
Ginko
Um, a concert, a live show, and an opera, right?
Kozue
Yes. In any case, I want to take in all kinds of stimulation from outside. It will be a hard schedule, but please bear with me.
Ginko
Yes, it's all right. And, um... Well...
Ginko
Actually, I made lunch for today.
Kozue
Oh... You did, Ginko-san?
Ginko
Yes. I wanted to cheer you up somehow, Kozue-senpai, so I consulted everyone in the club.
Kozue
Kosuzu-san and Hime-san, perhaps?
Ginko
No... If I tell those two, they get amused and tease me one time out of three...
Kozue
You get along so well. As expected of Hasu no Sho-Sankaku.
Ginko
I-it's not like that. So I asked the senpais. Then...
Sayaka
In that case, eating delicious food is best.
Rurino
Yeah, yeah! If you want, maybe you could make her a homemade lunch?
Sayaka
That would be lovely. If so, I will help too.
Ginko
So, if it isn't a bother.
Kozue
...I'm happy, Ginko-san.
Kozue
That part of you, where you carefully prepare in advance and get various things ready, is truly reliable.
Ginko
Ah... Thank you very much.
Ginko
Well then, um...
Ginko
Today, let's do our best together with Big Sister Ginko, okay, Kozue-chan?
Kozue
Wait. What was that...?
Ginko
D-did something happen? Kozue-chan.
Kozue
Come to think of it, you said you got advice from "the senpais," didn't you? Don't tell me.
Ginko
Yes. I asked Tsuzuri-senpai and Megumi-senpai too!
Megumi
You want to cheer Kozue up, huh?
Tsuzuri
Come to think of it, Kozu once said she wanted an older sister and called Ruri "Big Sister Rurino."
Megumi
That's great☆ Ginko-chan, you should be her big sister for the day too☆
Ginko
They said.
Kozue
Ginko-san, I'm happy about your kindness. But I'm all right.
Ginko
Ah... Megumi-senpai said, "If Kozue says she's 'all right,' it just means she's embarrassed☆"...
Kozue
Sigh... I should have warned you sooner, Ginko-san. You have been teased by two very hopeless upperclassmen.
Ginko
No... Those two would do something like that...?
Kozue
They do. To me.
Ginko
Ah... You are close, after all. As expected of Hasu no Dai-Sankaku.
Kozue
Let's end this topic here. I feel like I'll be exhausted before we even go to the concert.
Ginko
Um... Then, big sister for the day?
Kozue
You don't have to do that!
Ginko
That was such a beautiful performance.
Kozue
Yes, the interplay between the flute and oboe was especially exquisite. The violin solo sounded as if it were singing.
Kozue
There seem to be parts I can make use of in composition too... I'm sorry, may I take a few notes?
Ginko
Yes, of course.
Kozue
Phew... Sorry to keep you waiting.
Ginko
Not at all. I was just ready myself.
Kozue
My... What a lovely lunch.
Kozue
Rice balls, nikujaga, kinpira burdock. Tamagoyaki, and is this agedashi tofu?
Ginko
Yes. I heard that you like tofu, Kozue-senpai.
Kozue
It must have been difficult to make all this.
Ginko
Sayaka-senpai and Rurino-senpai helped me too.
Ginko
Since coming to Hasunosora, it's been a while since I really cooked. Ah, but at the end of last year, I made osechi with my grandmother.
Kozue
I see... Is your teacher well?
Ginko
Yes! She isn't very honest with her words, but ever since July, she's become completely absorbed in the School Idol Club.
Kozue
Hehe... That makes me proud.
Kozue
...I wonder if winning Love Live! let me repay her even a little.
Ginko
I think many people learned Hasunosora's name!
Kozue
If so, then all the more reason we cannot do anything unsightly at the Renge Festival.
Kozue
I have to overcome this slump properly and complete the song...
Ginko
Ah... Next is the rock band's live show, right? It's a culture I don't usually come into contact with either, so I'm looking forward to it!
Kozue
...Yes. Once we've enjoyed the lunch, shall we head to the next place?
Ginko
Yes. We have to line up for merchandise and buy T-shirts and towels too.
Kozue
Um... Ginko-san, do you often go to live shows?
Ginko
No, never before. I researched thoroughly for today.
Kozue
I see... You truly are serious and a good girl...
Ginko
Today was... somehow, such a wonderful day...
Kozue
We went to a live show and then an opera in a row. Aren't you tired?
Ginko
No... I think this too is an experience I never would have had if I hadn't met you at Hasunosora, Kozue-senpai.
Ginko
When I get back, I almost want to brag to Kaho-senpai.
Kozue
That sounds like it would get rather lively.
Ginko
It would. Kaho-senpai loves you, Kozue-senpai. Hehe.
Kozue
I received so much stimulation today too. Music comes in many forms, and expression does too...
Kozue
It seems I had forgotten something so obvious.
Ginko
You took a lot of notes too. After working this hard, I'm sure you'll be all right!
Kozue
Yes, I think so. I feel like I can finally see a light in the maze where I had been stuck.
Kozue
Being able to discuss our impressions together was a big part of it too. There were many things I wouldn't have noticed alone.
Kozue
Thank you for going along with me.
Ginko
If I was useful, then I'm glad!
Kozue
Come to think of it, I never asked you properly. What will you do from now on, Ginko-san?
Kozue
You joined the club saying that winning Love Live! was your dream, didn't you?
Ginko
Ah, yes.
Ginko
For me, hmm...
Ginko
Unlike you, Kozue-senpai, I was aiming for Love Live! in order to make Hasunosora and Kanazawa's traditional culture known to the world.
Ginko
So I think what I do from here on won't change. If anything, now that Hasunosora is receiving attention, this is the time to work hard.
Kozue
I see.
Ginko
How about you, Kozue-senpai? How does it feel to have won Love Live! and made your dream come true?
Kozue
...Of course, it feels wonderful.
Kozue
It was a dream we seized with everyone's strength.
Ginko
It's amazing, isn't it? To keep challenging it for three years, and then win in your final year.
Ginko
Truly, you're the aspiration of school idols all over the world.
Kozue
...Yes.
Kozue
Aspiration... But perhaps... that really is true.
Kozue
...Sigh.
Kozue
I still can't write...
Kozue
I experienced so much wonderful music, and Ginko-san went along with me so much...
Kozue
No matter how many days pass...
Kozue
I'm getting sick of this... I'm so close to graduation...
Kozue
I at least want to leave a song for the girls who saved me...
Kozue
...Sigh.
Kozue
...Come in.
Kaho
Kozue-senpai!
Kozue
...What is it? It's already lights out.
Kozue
Did you hear from Ginko-san and come here out of jealousy?
Kaho
No, I didn't!
Kaho
I thought seriously about this too! A secret plan to do something about your slump, Kozue-senpai!
Kaho
Kozue-senpai, please come with me now!
Kozue
Now...? What about your health?
Kaho
I'm completely fine now!
Kaho
You might not know because you've been shut in your room composing, but I've even returned to practice!
Kozue
Then that's good... But what are we going to do?
Kaho
These past days, you've been busy the whole time, right?
Kaho
Starting with the Rindo Festival and the Love Live! preliminaries, then the Big Voice Championship, Megumi-senpai's fierce battle, the Hokuriku Tournament, and the Love Live! finals...
Kozue
Y-yes, that's true.
Kaho
And next month is the Renge Festival!
Kaho
We have to make the song that will culminate 104th-term Cerise Bouquet, and these have been days when you couldn't relax at all.
Kaho
Anyone would get tired from that, even you, Kozue-senpai!
Kozue
D-do you think so...?
Kaho
Yes. I know.
Kaho
You're just a little more hardworking than other people, Kozue-senpai. You're not Superman or anything. You're the same as us.
Kaho
So, let's go!
Kozue
Go where?
Kaho
Tonight, leave every single burden you're carrying behind.
Kaho
Let's elope, you and me!
Kozue
E-elope...?
