PART 2

Kaho
Whaaat!?
Sayaka
Is that true...!?
Rurino
Mizukawa is really... coming back!?
Izumi
Yes. Today, Ceras's junior by one year, Tateshina Kairi-san, came and told us.
Ceras
She told us!
Izumi
Strictly speaking, it isn't a revival.
Ceras
Strictly speaking, it isn't!
Izumi
The former corporation has already withdrawn.
Izumi
But a new educational corporation is apparently planning to receive approval to establish a school on the sold campus grounds and recruit students.
Izumi
The managing body will change, but the school building and name will remain the same. To the public, it will likely be taken as "Mizukawa's revival."
Kosuzu
In other words... in other words!?
Hime
Is it like a convenience store being built again where the same convenience store used to be?
Izumi
Well, something like that.
Izumi
They seem to have reflected on the management failures, and this time, rather than an integrated junior and senior high, they'll restart on a smaller scale as an ordinary high school.
Ginko
The plans have gone that far...?
Izumi
Of course, normally there is no way a plan could move this quickly.
Izumi
But the Zuiren Festival and the Love Live! finals playoff caught a great many eyes, and the voices of people supporting them reached the right places. It seems things are moving exceptionally.
Ceras
And! According to Kairi, Mizukawa's revival has already entered the countdown stage!
Kaho & Sayaka & Rurino & Ginko & Kosuzu & Hime
!
Sayaka
I see...
Ceras
I was really, really surprised too...!
Izumi
Mizukawa was a school one could call a symbol of the region.
Izumi
By the time people realized its value, the closure had already been decided, but support came together belatedly.
Izumi
The story of "reclaiming a lost alma mater" is easy to understand and beautiful. Drawing up a reconstruction plan was probably more realistic than building a new school.
Izumi
So former Mizukawa students, including Kairi-san, wondered if there was any way they could support that reconstruction plan,
Izumi
and they brought the matter to us.
Izumi
In other words.
Izumi
They want to hold a "Second Zuiren Festival," and asked if we would cooperate.
Kaho
A Second Zuiren Festival...!
Rurino
I see... Mizukawa's name really spread because of what happened at the Love Live! national tournament. And because of Zuiren Festival's reputation too.
Izumi
Exactly.
Kaho
That's great! Secchan, Izumi-chan!
Kaho
Let's do the Second Zuiren Festival! And this time, let's bring Mizukawa back for sure!
Kaho
The Mizukawa the two of you wanted to protect!
Ceras
Yeah!
Ceras
I can't believe an opportunity like this came along. I'm really surprised... But I'm happy. So... so happy.
Sayaka
...Yes, it does feel deeply moving.
Sayaka
Looking back now, the flow from Zuiren Festival to the Love Live! playoff
Sayaka
was no exaggeration to call an event that became the impetus for holding Bloom Garden Party.
Rurino
It really was.
Rurino
Helping Izumi-chan and Ceras-chan bloom grew into something bigger,
Rurino
and connected to Kaho-chan's "stage for making every person bloom."
Kaho
Yeah, yeah!
Ginko
If we hadn't had that experience, I feel like I couldn't have pictured Bloom Days succeeding either.
Ginko
I might have thought it was impossible...
Hime
It was a major turning point for the School Idol Club too. I always remember it and use it as a reference when holding events.
Kosuzu
Back then, Kachimachi was still a peeping chick who didn't know left from right, but now I should be able to help more!
Kaho
Then, everyone!
Sayaka
But before that, there is one extremely large problem...
Kaho
Huh!?
Sayaka
The schedule.
Kaho
W-We can make it work... right...!?
Sayaka
As you know, Kaho-san, preparations for Starring Bloom are finally in the home stretch.
Sayaka
This week is packed tight with meetings with school idol clubs from other schools too...
Ginko
Right... Sayaka-senpai and Rurino-senpai obviously don't have time to go to Nagano in the immediate future.
Kaho
Uuugh!!
Rurino
Ruri actually still can't use the clone technique, you know...
Izumi
I understand perfectly well that this is the busiest period. From here on, Ceras and I will manage somehow.
Ceras
Y-Yeah. Just being allowed to use Hasunosora's name is a grateful blessing!
Kaho
But that doesn't mean we should have to choose one or the other!
Ginko
Which is why.
Ginko
We'll go to Nagano with them. Please leave this to us 104th-term students.
Kaho & Rurino
Oh!?
Ceras
Ginko-senpai...!?
Ginko
If the 103rd-term students really do find time, please come help. When that happens, we'll be counting on you.
Hime
Oooh... After getting through Bloom Days, Ginko-chan has really grown up...
Hime
I'm obviously okay with it. And Kosuzu-chan... well, I don't even need to ask, huh?
Kosuzu
Yeah! Sounds good! It's a really wonderful story! It's the most chest of all! Chest!
Hime
My, my, what a free and fine chest that was.
Ceras
Senpai...
Rurino
Kaho-chan, let's leave this to the second-years. It'll be okay. If it's them, I'm sure we don't need to worry.
Rurino
Hime, Ginko-chan, and Kosuzu-chan have all grown so much this year.
Rurino
We're the ones who know that best, right?
Rurino
This is probably the School Idol Club's full strength as it is right now.
Kaho
Yeah... you're right. Then we have to work hard for the second-years' share too!
Sayaka
Then let us all do our best for the Second Zuiren Festival.
Ceras
Yeah!
Kaho
Aim for Mizukawa's revival! Ei, ei, oh!
Sayaka & Rurino & Ginko & Kosuzu & Hime & Ceras
Oh!
Izumi
...
Izumi
Goodness.
Izumi
That was quite surprising news.
Ceras
"Surprising" doesn't even cover it! We have to give it everything we've got!
Izumi
...I see. Yes, you're right.
Izumi
I'll do my best to support you, Ceras.
Ceras
...Aren't you happy, Izumi? About Mizukawa coming back.
Izumi
To me, that story already ended.
Izumi
I couldn't save Mizukawa. I did have regrets. But my story with Mizukawa ended there.
Ceras
Huh...?
Izumi
Seeing everyone in the School Idol Club fired up made me realize it all over again.
Izumi
...I'm different from them.
Izumi
It should, by rights, be more personal to me than anyone. Yet even signs of Mizukawa's revival don't become a motive that drives me.
Izumi
There is no passion welling up from my chest.
Izumi
...It feels empty. Truly. That all I remember is regret.
Ceras
...
Izumi
...No, I'm sorry. I didn't mean to throw cold water on your excitement.
Ceras
Then it's "revenge"!
Izumi
What?
Ceras
For me and for you! This is our chance for revenge!
Ceras
You were frustrated, right!? Then let's take that frustration and this time bring Mizukawa back for sure!
Ceras
If you can work hard because you feel frustrated, that proves you're not empty, doesn't it!?
Izumi
...Proof that I am not empty...
Ceras
Yeah! Look, Izumi, you've made so many people's dreams come true until now. I'm sure you've hardly ever failed.
Ceras
Then I think you can work hard with a different kind of fuel.
Izumi
...But.
Izumi
Lately, I've also been able to engage in school idol activities with high motivation.
Izumi
Yet there is also the possibility that this will only prove my emptiness all over again.
Ceras
Th-That might be true, but...
Izumi
Again and again, no matter how many times, I find I cannot become like you all...
Ceras
Even so! If you don't challenge it, you won't get failure or success!
Ceras
S-Sorry.
Izumi
...No, you're right.
Izumi
...Last month, I was allowed to poke my nose a little into Kosuzu-san and Sayaka-senpai's circumstances.
Izumi
Even if you think you're wrong, try moving forward anyway. I did think, with certainty, that this is important.
Ceras
...Then!
Izumi
I don't mind a general opinion. I'd like to ask you... Is revenge something that "fires you up"?
Ceras
Absolutely!
Ceras
For example, even Hasunosora, who beat us Mizukawa in the playoff, had missed winning the national tournament in the finals the year before.
Ceras
Then for a whole year, Hana-chan and everyone must have worked really hard, using that frustration as a springboard!
Ceras
I did too... When I was a child, I was hospitalized, and I couldn't make my dream of becoming a violinist come true.
Ceras
That's exactly why I decided to chase my dream of being a school idol with everything I had.
Ceras
"Frustration" has that much power!
Izumi
...I see. I understand.
Izumi
Thank you, Ceras. Then I, too, will stake myself on this opportunity.
Ceras
Yeah! This is our "revenge challenge"!
Izumi
Yes.
Izumi
...Of course, I do think it's important to fulfill the feelings of those who wish for Mizukawa's revival, and of many other people.
Izumi
But for this time only, I want to devote my strength to fulfilling my own inner cry.
Izumi
Though it is a rather selfish thing to say.
Ceras
It's not, Izumi. If that's your dream, I'll absolutely support it!
Izumi
...Thank you.
Izumi
Then, while we're at it, there is one favor I'd like to ask of you.
Ceras
Yeah, okay! Say anything!
Izumi
This is revenge to regain my passion. So, what do you think?
Izumi
Until this matter is over, may I once again treat you like a princess?
Ceras
Huh... I-Is that what you want to do, Izumi?
Izumi
Yes, very much. I want to properly revive the feelings and resolve I had back then.
Ceras
Izumi... it doesn't look like you're teasing me. You're serious.
Izumi
I'm glad my feelings came across.
Ceras
...Yeah, okay. That's fine.
Izumi
Thank you.
Ceras
But... That doesn't mean my relationship with you is going back to before April of this year.
Izumi
? What do you mean?
Ceras
...Nothing!
Izumi
Hmm... At any rate, I'm counting on you.
Ceras
...Yeah. I'm counting on you too, Izumi. My knight. Once more, to make my dream come true... lend me your strength.
Izumi
Of course. This time, I will see it through. My Princess.
Izumi
...Whew.
Izumi
Let's put the plan together at once and send it to Kairi-san. Now that we've decided to do this, we must challenge it seriously.
Izumi
But...
Izumi
Even so, if nothing is born within me... then this time, that will truly be the end.
Izumi
Starring Bloom is next month. And once that is over, the real event will finally arrive. Bloom Garden Party is waiting.
Izumi
Ceras. The time left to you and me is far less than the numbers make it appear.
Izumi
So if this "revenge" ends in futility...
Izumi
Next year, I will leave Hasunosora cleanly and let go of your hand. I am sure that will be best for both of us.
Izumi
...Ah, how I hate this... For that reason too, I must give everything I have...!
Izumi
Yes...! I will stake all of myself and seize it... my own... "passion"...!
