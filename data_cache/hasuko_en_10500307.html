PART 7

Kaho
Good work, Sayaka-chan. You're working hard.
Sayaka
Yes, thanks to you.
Sayaka
Kaho-san.
Kaho
Hmm?
Sayaka
Please allow me to apologize again.
Sayaka
The opportunity you found after working so hard you harmed your health... I let it slip away while you were absent.
Kaho
I told you it wasn't your fault. It isn't like everything would have been fine if I were there.
Sayaka
That isn't...
Sayaka
No. Even so, the reason we can face forward is entirely thanks to you, Kaho-san.
Sayaka
I merely wanted to thank you.
Kaho
... Thanks to me, huh.
Sayaka
Yes. So, what have you thought of this time?
Kaho
...If I thought of something, would you come along?
Sayaka
Yes, of course.
Kaho
Because my ideas always turn in a good direction?
Sayaka
Yes.
Kaho
I'm going to enter Love Live!
Sayaka
...Huh?
Kaho
Love Live! is a place where dreams can come true.
Kaho
I'll stand there once more and shout my dream again on that stage.
Kaho
Then I'm sure there will be someone who offers us a stage, like with this rock festival.
Sayaka
...
Kaho
So, Sayaka-chan, I want you to lead everyone from here.
Kaho
Helping everyone this time made me realize it. Everyone knows how they can bloom.
Kaho
Then, yes.
Kaho
Now that my dream has become everyone's dream, my part regarding the stage where everyone can bloom itself is already done.
Sayaka
W-Wait a moment!
Sayaka
That sounds as though you are saying you won't appear in the stage where everyone can bloom...!
Kaho
That's right.
Sayaka
!
Kaho
My dream is already everyone's dream.
Kaho
So everyone's dream can come true, I want to do what I can.
Kaho
That means entering Love Live! So... I'll entrust the stage where everyone can bloom to Ginko-chan and the others... to next year.
Sayaka
Kaho-san! Even if you enter Love Live!, the situation will not change like that...!
Sayaka
If that could obtain us a stage, then you, who already won Love Live!, should have been able to achieve it by asking!
Kaho
That...
Kaho
Then it doesn't have to be Love Live! Next year's rock festival, an amazing musical, anything.
Kaho
I'll go somewhere people don't know us, put all my strength into it, and find someone who will help my dream.
Kaho
In any case, I'll do what I can for everyone. Otherwise, the dream won't reach.
Sayaka
Kaho-san!!
Sayaka
Didn't you say it yourself? That because we aim for something different, we won't enter Love Live!
Sayaka
That it would be fulfilled not alone, but together! If you are not there, it has no meaning!
Kaho
I know. I know that.
Kaho
But if I can't reach it myself, then I at least want to do what I can for it...
Sayaka
Then let's think again! You are sure to come up with a good idea, Kaho-san!
Sayaka
And then I will always support you...
Kaho
That's exactly it!!
Kaho
That my ideas will always turn out well...!
Kaho
I can't believe that myself anymore...!!
Sayaka
Ah...
Kaho
I realized my dream isn't just my dream! I was happy! That's already enough!
Kaho
If you say you'll support me... then listen to me, Sayaka-chan!!
Sayaka
I see.
Sayaka
It is true that I only listened to what you said. I am the one who made you like this.
Kaho
! What! Like if you take everything on yourself, that's fine...!
Sayaka
Aren't you the same, Kaho-san...! What do you want more than fulfilling your own dream yourself!!
Kaho
For everyone to bloom!!
Kaho
Ever since I joined the School Idol Club, I've wanted to bloom together with everyone.
Kaho
That feeling grew stronger and stronger... and then I thought: if everyone can bloom, I don't have to be at the center.
Kaho
If I can help their dreams, that's enough. I really thought about it.
Kaho
I'll do it. I won't let you do it, Sayaka-chan. If you don't bloom, Sayaka-chan, then it has no meaning.
Sayaka
...
Kaho
Do you understand?
Sayaka
But...
Kaho
Please understand... please. I... I...
Kaho
I... this is all I can do. The "real me."
Sayaka
...!!
Sayaka
...I'm sorry.
Kaho
...
Sayaka
Excuse me...
Kaho
Sayaka-chan...
Sayaka
I... I...
Sayaka
If you are not smiling, Kaho-san... I cannot possibly bloom...
Kaho
...!
Sayaka
I am so pathetic for being unable to do anything for you... for not having done anything...
Kaho
Even if you say that...
Sayaka
What... have I been doing?
Sayaka
I said with my mouth that I would support Kaho-san...
Sayaka
But I placed everything about what we should do entirely on Kaho-san.
Sayaka
Of course she would be crushed... by that... What was I even...
Sayaka
What Kaho-san says isn't wrong.
Sayaka
We search for people who sympathize with our full effort.
Sayaka
Like the rock festival, places where we might be allowed to use a stage, competitions... places like that, and make our move...
Sayaka
Think. If I can't even do this, then I truly have no qualification to be president...
Rurino
There you are.
Sayaka
Rurino-san.
Rurino
I searched for you. You and Kaho-chan both weren't replying at all.
Sayaka
I'm sorry, I didn't notice...
Rurino
It's fine, it's fine. I found you like this.
Rurino
But... did something happen?
Sayaka
Yes...
Rurino
Just like you and Kaho-chan are double presidents, Ruri has the role of supporting you two.
Rurino
Tell me what happened. Kaho-chan wouldn't say anything.
Sayaka
You saw Kaho-san too. ...Well, we had a slight difference in direction.
Rurino
Don't say it like a band about to break up.
Sayaka
...
Rurino
Wait, seriously???
Sayaka
It is not a breakup. Well, I'll manage somehow.
Rurino
Don't say you'll manage somehow.
Rurino
...Sayaka-chan. Tell me. The people Ruri wants to support include the presidents too.
Sayaka
...You are kind, Rurino-san.
Sayaka
But... that is exactly why I do not want you to hear this.
Rurino
Huh...
Sayaka
What I should do, what I want to do. I have sorted that out.
Sayaka
So it's all right. Please trust me and leave it to me. If I think to rely on you, I will.
Rurino
...
Rurino
Kaho-chan told me the same thing. "Because Rurino-chan is kind."
Sayaka
...I see.
Rurino
Am I that unreliable?
Sayaka
N-No, that isn't it! Only, if I told you, you would push yourself...
Rurino
That's exactly...!
Kosuzu
Sayaka-senpaaai!!!
Sayaka
Kosuzu-san.
Rurino
Everyone.
Kosuzu
Ah! Were you in the middle of talking?
Sayaka
No...
Rurino
Did something... good happen?
Kosuzu
Ah, yes! I decided to do what Kachimachi thinks too.
Kosuzu
So... I talked to Hime-chan and the others about this event.
Kosuzu
I thought it was lonely. For the rock festival we all worked hard on to end as "challenge failed!"
Hime
Hello. I'm part of the group inspired by Kosuzu-chan. Investigating the cause of defeat is what a gamer ought to do.
Ginko
Sayaka-senpai, you said we were wrong from the start... but that was not true.
Sayaka
What... do you mean?
Ceras
Maybe we did lack preparation... but setting that aside, we properly included everything we wanted to do.
Ceras
If none of that got through at all, I thought I would still be depressed.
Ceras
I wanted to properly check once whether that was really true.
Kosuzu
So... we asked!
Kosuzu
There were almost no impressions of Hasunosora, but we found people on social media who went to the rock festival... and asked them one after another, "How were Kachimachi and the others!?"
Rurino
Eeeeeh!?
Ceras
It was extremely hard.
Sayaka
I imagine so...
Izumi
For that, everyone looks happy.
Kosuzu
Because it had meaning!! There were people who properly told us they had fun!!
Kosuzu
For Kachimachi and the others, it wasn't an event with no value! We properly received messages!
Kosuzu
This person, this person, and this person. They said they might be interested in Hasunosora from now on!
Sayaka
...Kosuzu-san.
Ginko
What do you think, Sayaka-senpai? By exchanging words with many people alongside Kosuzu, I felt like I want to try again.
Sayaka
...
Kosuzu
Aw... was it meaningless after all...?
Sayaka
No, no.
Sayaka
I also... felt that I want to try again. And... I want her to feel that she wants to try again.
Izumi
Sayaka-senpai? Did something...
Sayaka
What matters is feeling. I should have known that from the start.
Sayaka
And yet I evaluated only actions and results... I forgot what mattered most.
Rurino
...
Sayaka
I'm sorry, everyone. I'm going to see Kaho-san for a bit!
Rurino
Ah... yeah. Sayaka-chan.
Sayaka
Yes.
Rurino
Do your best.
Sayaka
Yes.
