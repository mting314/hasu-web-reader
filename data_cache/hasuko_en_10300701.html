PART 1

Kaho
One million people, Sayaka-chan.
Sayaka
Huh? What is this all of a sudden? One...?
Kaho
One million people!!
Kaho
We're going to perform live for one million people! That's our goal!
Sayaka
Th-that is...
Kaho
Don't you think it's amazing!?
Sayaka
...Wouldn't that be difficult?
Kaho
Ugh, well, maybe, but... But I think goals are better when they're big and hard!
Sayaka
Um, Kaho-san.
Kaho
Is it too much for us after all...!?
Sayaka
No, that is not what I mean... No matter how large a dome is, it cannot hold that many people.
Kaho
W-well, that's, you know.
Sayaka
?
Kaho
We squeeze them in tight.
Sayaka
Are you mistaking people for some all-you-can-stuff bargain item!?
Sayaka
I do think that having a goal itself is a very wonderful thing, but...
Kaho
Mmm, no good, huh.
Sayaka
Now then, big goals are all well and good, Kaho-san.
Sayaka
Our seniors are not here today.
Kaho
Oh, ah, right! That was today! I was wondering why they weren't coming.
Kaho
I thought maybe they were being attacked by tanuki...
Sayaka
I cannot really imagine the two of them losing to tanuki...
Sayaka
In any case, it means there are things we ourselves have to do.
Kaho
Umm...
Sayaka
...I hope this is not the case, but have you forgotten?
Kaho
N-no no no, of course not!? I remember perfectly! Today, Kozue-senpai is absent because of the all-club meeting!
Kaho
Tsuzuri-senpai is also... Umm, absent because she's talking with a teacher!
Sayaka
Yes. Tsuzuri-senpai is apparently having a career counseling meeting. It seems our school holds them regularly from the latter half of first year.
Sayaka
And so, correct answer, Kaho-san. This is what we need to do.
Kaho
Ah, right! The request from the newspaper club that Kozue-senpai mentioned!
Sayaka
Yes. It is an article introducing our club. There is no particular deadline, but...
Sayaka
Since the purpose is to post it at the Nadeshiko Festival, the sooner the better.
Kaho
The first-years have to write it, right?
Sayaka
It is not absolutely required, but apparently it is tradition for new students to write the newspaper articles for the Nadeshiko Festival.
Sayaka
That is why Otomune-senpai said she was looking forward to our article.
Kaho
I see, so we have to do our best for Kozue-senpai too!
Sayaka
Yes. We must not bring shame to our seniors' names. It will also be our first culture festival, after all.
Sayaka
In other words, what I want to say is this.
Kaho
Is...?
Sayaka
The Nadeshiko Festival has already begun!
Kaho
Whaaat!? Where!? Where!?
Sayaka
Here, of course!
Kaho
H-here...!
Sayaka
The quality of the article we create will directly affect how the School Idol Club is evaluated at the Nadeshiko Festival!
Kaho
Th-that's true...!
Sayaka
So, for this club introduction article. In other words, let us write about the appeal of this School Idol Club as we see it!!!
Kaho
Leave it to me!
Kaho
First, lives are fun! Practice is tough, but it's fun too! And being together with everyone in the School Idol Club is fun!
Kaho
Spending time together with all the people cheering us on, and then that feeling afterward when all your strength leaves you like, "It's over!"
Kaho
The songs are pretty and cute and fun too...
Sayaka
W-wait a moment, please. At this rate, we will run out of space just listing headings!
Kaho
Whaa!? You're right! It's nowhere near enough!!
Sayaka
I am not the one who decided the size.
Sayaka
It is actually a fairly large size, though...
Kaho
That just means the Hasunosora School Idol Club is that appealing!
Sayaka
Y-yes. Of course it is. However...
Sayaka
If we cram everything in with tiny letters after all, I think it will become an article that is hard for everyone to understand.
Kaho
What should we do, Sayaka-chan?
Sayaka
Shall we narrow the content down a little? I did ask everyone in class about it, and it seems many clubs are summarizing their achievements from last year.
Sayaka
So I was thinking that we could also go to the large storage room and research various things.
Kaho
Last year's achievements, huh.
Kaho
But, Sayaka-chan. The kids in the other clubs are doing that kind of thing, right?
Sayaka
Huh? Ah, yes. That is right.
Kaho
Then, then, let's do something different! I think an introduction article only the Hasunosora School Idol Club can make would be good!
Sayaka
That is... certainly true.
Kaho
And I want Kozue-senpai, who went out of her way to ask us, to know how much we love this place! I'm sure she'd be happy!
Sayaka
Understood. Then, something that only this Hasunosora School Idol Club has...
Sayaka
And that is also an appealing point anyone can understand. Let us search for that.
Kaho
Heh heh heh.
Sayaka
Kaho-san?
Kaho
There's no need to search for that, Sayaka-kun.
Sayaka
Sayaka-kun.
Kaho
The answer is... our senpai!!
Kaho
First, Kozue-senpai is... dependable!!
Sayaka
I see.
Kaho
When I'm with her, I feel at ease, and onstage, just because I'm together with Kozue-senpai, I can feel incredibly free!
Sayaka
Mm-hmm.
Kaho
During practice she's a little scary, and that serious feeling she has as club president makes me think, I need to get it together too, but...
Kaho
But that is also why I feel like everything will be okay as long as Kozue-senpai is there!
Sayaka
I see... yes. I think that sounds good.
Kaho
Really!?
Sayaka
Yes, Kaho-san, you really love Otomune-senpai, don't you?
Kaho
Ehehe. I have to make sure everyone else feels that too!
Sayaka
That is right!
Kaho
Then you too, Sayaka-chan, without getting embarrassed like usual, talk about Tsuzuri-senpai! Go on!
Sayaka
Understood. Though I will say, she is not someone reliable like Otomune-senpai.
Kaho
Ahaha. Sayaka-chan, you're always kind of strict with Tsuzuri-senpai.
Sayaka
Still, Tsuzuri-senpai's performance ability, which more than makes up for that, is something no one else could ever imitate.
Kaho
I see?
Sayaka
The sight of Tsuzuri-senpai onstage, captivating anyone at a glance, may indeed have been lonely for Tsuzuri-senpai.
Sayaka
But I am here now, so I will make that all right. In other words, Tsuzuri-senpai's own perceived weakness is gone, and she is invincible.
Kaho
...Wait, did you always go this far, Sayaka-chan?
Sayaka
Once she steps off the stage, she is someone with many absentminded moments, but that too is one part of what forms Tsuzuri-senpai's charm.
Sayaka
Even during practice, once she starts dancing, it is as if a switch flips, and with a single movement she captivates everyone... A true school idol.
Kaho
Sayaka-chan? Sayaka-chaaan?
Sayaka
In other words, as long as Tsuzuri-senpai is here, the Hasunosora School Idol Club will never show any sign of decline.
Sayaka
When you think of the Hasunosora School Idol Club, you think of Tsuzuri-senpai's...
Kaho
Okay, okay, okay! The Hasunosora School Idol Club is what it is because Kozue-senpai is here!
Sayaka
? Umm?
Kaho
Tsuzuri-senpai is definitely amazing! But I think when you think of the Hasunosora School Idol Club, you think of Kozue-senpai!
Sayaka
I see...
Kaho
Yep, yep.
Sayaka
Everyone is free to have their own way of thinking.
Kaho
Huhhh!? K-Kozue-senpai is the club president, she supports everyone, and at lives she's the center of everyone's attention!
Sayaka
It is true that Tsuzuri-senpai is not the club president, and if anything, she is on the side being supported, but if we look solely at the point of being a school idol, Tsuzuri-senpai is the face of this club.
Sayaka
There is a beauty only Tsuzuri-senpai can express.
Kaho
Guh, ugh... L-look, Kozue-senpai is relied on inside and outside the club, and she's at the center of the School Idol Club!
Sayaka
...Tsuzuri-senpai is asked by everyone to perform a song during breaks!
Kaho
And, um... she's really good at studying too!
Sayaka
Th-that is... wait, how is Tsuzuri-senpai in that regard? Somehow I feel like she might not be good.
Kaho
And she's athletic too!
Sayaka
She is a school idol, so everyone has at least the minimum! Though I do not know how good Tsuzuri-senpai's grades in PE are!
Kaho
And she's super popular with everyone around her too!
Sayaka
Guh... H-how are you going to prove that! Tsuzuri-senpai is too!
Kaho
Prove it!? Umm, that's...!
Sayaka
Tsuzuri-senpai probably, you know, has captivated everyone at lives since she was a first-year...
Kaho
"Probably, you know" is super vague! That's exactly what needs proof!
Sayaka
I cannot prove it! I cannot... but.
Kaho & Sayaka
...
Kaho
...Hey, Sayaka-chan.
Sayaka
...Yes. What is it, Kaho-san?
Kaho
About what we were talking about earlier.
Kaho
Do you know when Tsuzuri-senpai started getting asked by everyone to dance for them and stuff?
Sayaka
No... Kaho-san, do you know what made Otomune-senpai start being relied on by those around her?
Kaho
No. ...Actually, even the lives I mentioned earlier... I don't know what Kozue-senpai and the others were doing last year.
Sayaka
Just as I said earlier, with us as we are now, we cannot state clearly whether they were active since first year or not.
Kaho
Yeah. ...We don't know as much about our senpai as we thought, huh?
Sayaka
That is true. Since this is such a good opportunity... I would like to learn various things about our seniors from before we knew them.
Kaho
Yeah, let's learn about our senpai! Then we'll put out a big article and make them happy!
